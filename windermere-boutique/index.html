The Place · Windermere · Lake District

Between Windermere and Bowness

The hotel stands on Lake Road, halfway between Windermere village and the lakeshore at Bowness-on-Windermere.

Windermere is England’s largest natural lake, about ten and a half miles long, ringed by the fells of the Lake District National Park.

The shops, restaurants and steamer piers of Bowness Bay are an easy walk; Windermere station is just up the hill.
