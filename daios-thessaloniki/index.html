The Place · Nikis Avenue · Thessaloniki

On the Thermaic Gulf

Daios stands on Nikis Avenue, the seafront promenade of Thessaloniki, looking across the Thermaic Gulf towards Mount Olympus on clear days.

Aristotelous Square, the port and the shopping streets are a few minutes away on foot; the White Tower stands at the end of the promenade.

The Rotunda, the Byzantine churches and the upper town are all within walking distance.
