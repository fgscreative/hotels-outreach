The Place · Stari Grad · Hvar

On Hvar

Stari Grad lies at the head of a long bay on the north coast of Hvar, founded by Greeks from Paros in 384 BC.

Behind it, the Stari Grad Plain still keeps its ancient Greek field divisions — a UNESCO site since 2008 — among olives, vines and lavender.

The founders, Majda and Dalibor, chose the villas one by one.
