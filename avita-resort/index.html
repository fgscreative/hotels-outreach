The Place · Bad Tatzmannsdorf · Burgenland

In southern Burgenland

AVITA stands on Thermenplatz in Bad Tatzmannsdorf, a spa town in the hills of southern Burgenland, near the Hungarian border.

The region is known for its mild climate and for Uhudler, a wine made from old hybrid vines found almost only here.

Vienna is about an hour by road; Graz around an hour and a quarter.
