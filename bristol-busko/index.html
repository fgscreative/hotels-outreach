The Place · Spa Park · Busko-Zdrój

By the Spa Park

Busko-Zdrój has treated guests every season since 1 June 1836, with sulphide water drawn from the Winiarska Forest.

Bristol is a late-19th-century Art Nouveau villa with a dome and spire beside the Spa Park, with a modern wing behind it.

Its owners, the artists Dorota and Tomasz Tworek, have filled it with their collection.
