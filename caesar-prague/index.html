The Place · Nové Město · Prague 1

In the New Town

Hotel Caesar stands on Myslíkova in Nové Město, the New Town that Charles IV founded in 1348, a few streets back from the Vltava.

The Dancing House is about four hundred metres away; the National Theatre and the river embankment are a short walk.

Prague’s historic centre has been on the UNESCO World Heritage list since 1992.
