The Place · Malaucène · Mont Ventoux

Under Mont Ventoux

Malaucène is one of the three towns where the Ventoux climbs begin, on the mountain’s north-west side in the Vaucluse.

The summit, 1,910 m of bare white limestone, rises above forests of cedar, beech and pine.

Vaison-la-Romaine and its Roman ruins are ten minutes away; Avignon under an hour.
