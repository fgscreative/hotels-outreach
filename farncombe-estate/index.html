The Place · Broadway · The Cotswolds

Above Broadway

Farncombe lies high on the escarpment above Broadway, the honey-stone village at the northern edge of the Cotswolds.

Its 500 acres of forest and field are laced with trails and cycle routes, with Broadway Tower on the same ridge.

Cheltenham is about 40 minutes by car; Moreton-in-Marsh station, with direct trains to London, about 20.
