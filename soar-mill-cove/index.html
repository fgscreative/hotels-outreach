The Place · Soar Mill Cove · South Devon

Near Salcombe

Soar Mill Cove lies in a valley on the South Devon coast west of Salcombe, in a protected landscape of headlands and coves.

National Trust land runs for miles on either side, and the South West Coast Path passes above the cove between Bolt Head and Bolt Tail.

Salcombe is about ten minutes by car; Kingsbridge around fifteen, and Plymouth under an hour.
