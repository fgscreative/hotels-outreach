The Place · Sierra Nevada · Granada

In the Sierra Nevada

El Lodge sits at 2,100 m on the Maribel run above Pradollano, in Europe’s southernmost ski resort.

Above it rise the highest peaks in mainland Spain, Mulhacén at 3,479 m among them.

Granada and the Alhambra are 33 km down the mountain; Málaga airport about two hours.
