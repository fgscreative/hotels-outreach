The Place · Commeire · Orsières · Valais

A hamlet above the Val d'Entremont

Montagne Alternative is the hamlet of Commeire, on a sunny shelf above Orsières in the Val d'Entremont.

Its century-old barns, once used for hay and grain, have been rebuilt as rooms, kitchens and light-filled workspaces.

Lausanne is 1 h 20 by road and Geneva 2 h; the Great St Bernard Pass waits at the head of the valley.
