The Place · Royal Crescent · Bath

On the Crescent

John Wood the Younger designed the Royal Crescent: thirty houses behind one Palladian façade, 538 feet long, above Royal Victoria Park.

Bath is a World Heritage city, built on the only hot springs in Britain. No. 16 sits at the centre; No. 1, the museum, is two minutes’ walk away.

The crescent has played itself on screen, in Persuasion, The Duchess and Bridgerton.
