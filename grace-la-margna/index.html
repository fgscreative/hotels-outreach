The Place · St. Moritz · Engadin

Above Lake St. Moritz

GRACE La Margna stands on Via Serlas beside St. Moritz station, above the lake, at 1,856 metres in the Upper Engadin.

The La Margna wing was built in 1907 by the Engadin architect Nicolaus Hartmann; the Grace wing beside it opened in 2023.

The Corviglia funicular is a short walk uphill; the lake path and the Engadin’s cross-country trails start below the hotel.
