The Place · Newry Canal · County Down

On the Newry Canal

Canal Court stands on Merchants Quay beside the Newry Canal, Ireland’s oldest man-made waterway, opened in 1742.

The Mourne Mountains rise to the east and the Ring of Gullion to the west, inside a UNESCO Global Geopark.

Belfast and Dublin are each about an hour away.
