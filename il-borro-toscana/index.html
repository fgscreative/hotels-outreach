The Place · Valdarno · Tuscany

A borgo in the Valdarno

Il Borro is a medieval hamlet in the Valdarno, between Florence and Arezzo, restored by the Ferragamo family since 1993.

Its 1,100 hectares of vineyards, olive groves and woodland have been farmed organically since 2015.

Above it rises the Pratomagno ridge; along the valley stand the Balze, pillars of sand and clay carved by erosion.
