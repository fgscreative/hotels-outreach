The Place · Jesmond Dene · Newcastle

Beside Jesmond Dene

The house stands beside Jesmond Dene, the wooded valley of the Ouseburn that Lord Armstrong gave to the people of Newcastle in 1883.

Paths follow the river past waterfalls and the ruins of an old watermill, under beech, oak and yew.

Newcastle city centre is about five minutes by car; the Quayside and the Tyne bridges about ten.
