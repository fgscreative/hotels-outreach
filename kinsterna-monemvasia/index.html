The Place · Monemvasia · Peloponnese

Facing the rock

Kinsterna stands on the hillside at Agios Stefanos, looking across the bay to the fortified rock of Monemvasia.

Its name comes from the cistern at the heart of the mansion, which grew through Byzantine, Ottoman and Venetian times.

The causeway to the castle town is a short drive; Athens is about three and a half hours by car.
