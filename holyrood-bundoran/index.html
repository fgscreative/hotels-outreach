The Place · Bundoran · Donegal Bay

On Donegal Bay

The Holyrood stands on Main Street in Bundoran, Donegal’s most southerly town, above the Atlantic shore of Donegal Bay.

The McEniff family bought a four-bedroom B&B here in 1949, joined three houses into one hotel in 1962, and still run it.

A Blue Flag beach, the surf and a headland walk are on the doorstep.
