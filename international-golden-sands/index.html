The Place · Golden Sands · Varna

On Golden Sands

INTERNATIONAL stands on the front beach alley of Golden Sands, the long resort beach on the Black Sea north of Varna.

Behind the strip rises the forest of the Golden Sands Nature Park; Varna is about 17 kilometres to the south.

The tower reopened in 2013 after a full refurbishment and stays open all year.
