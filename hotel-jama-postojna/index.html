The Place · Postojna Cave Park · Slovenia

In the Slovenian karst

Hotel Jama stands in Postojna Cave Park, beside the entrance to Postojna Cave, about a kilometre from the town of Postojna.

Below it the Pivka river runs underground through the karst; inside, the cave holds a steady 10 °C all year.

Predjama Castle is about ten kilometres away; Ljubljana around forty minutes by road, Trieste about forty-five.
