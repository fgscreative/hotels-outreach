The Place · Old Town · Prague

Steps from Charles Bridge

BOOKQUET stands on Karoliny Světlé in Prague’s Old Town, a few steps from the Vltava and the Old Town end of Charles Bridge.

The house dates from the fifteenth century; inside, it has been carefully renovated in a modern style, with views of the river and Prague Castle.

Old Town Square, the National Theatre and Kampa island are all an easy walk away.
