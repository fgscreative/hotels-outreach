The Place · Lidingö · Stockholm archipelago

On Hustegafjärden

Skogshem & Wijk spreads over 20 hectares of waterfront on Lidingö, fifteen minutes from central Stockholm, beside the shipping lane.

Villa Skogshem was built in 1888 by the apothecary Gottfried Piltz and Villa Elinshäll in 1890; Anders Tengbom’s building opened in 1958.

Running trails leave from the door and join the Lidingöloppet track.
