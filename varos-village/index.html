The Place · Varos · Lemnos

On Lemnos

Varos lies on the central plain of Lemnos, a low island of golden hills and old volcanic stone, with Moudros bay and the ancient volcano in view.

The family restored the deserted village from 2010, house by house — the kafenio, the maternity clinic, the grain and cotton warehouses — in volcanic stone and Lemnian clay tiles.

Organic gardens and vineyards of Alexandrian muscat surround it; the airport is five minutes away.
