On the Rive Gauche

Esprit Saint Germain stands on rue Saint-Sulpice in the 6th arrondissement, between the Luxembourg Gardens and Boulevard Saint-Germain.

Place Saint-Sulpice and its fountain are a minute away; the cafés of Saint-Germain-des-Prés a few more.

The Seine, the Louvre and the Musée d’Orsay are all within a twenty-minute walk.
