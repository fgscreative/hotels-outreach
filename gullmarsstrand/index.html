The Place · Fiskebäckskil · Bohuslän

On the Gullmarn

Fiskebäckskil sits on the island of Skaftö in Bohuslän, a fishing village built on the herring boom of the 1700s.

The hotel stands on a pier at the mouth of the Gullmarn, Sweden’s only true threshold fjord, with Lysekil across the water.

Gothenburg is about an hour and a half by car; Oslo about two and a half.
