The Place · Lake Wolfgang · Salzkammergut

On Lake Wolfgang

Berau lies in its own bay on Lake Wolfgang, in the Salzkammergut lake district, between meadows, forest and mountains.

The lake is clear enough to drink from in places; above it rises the Schafberg, 1,783 m, climbed since 1893 by a steam cog railway from St. Wolfgang.

Bad Ischl is about twenty minutes by car; Salzburg around forty-five.
