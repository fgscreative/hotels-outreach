The Place · Karlovy Vary · Bohemia

In the Teplá valley

Carlsbad Plaza stands at the southern end of the spa quarter of Karlovy Vary, where the Teplá runs between wooded hills.

The town is built on hot mineral springs; the hottest, the Vřídlo, rises at about 73 °C. Guests drink the waters along the colonnades.

Since 2021 Karlovy Vary has been one of UNESCO’s Great Spa Towns of Europe.
