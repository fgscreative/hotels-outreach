On the Place des Vosges

The Pavillon stands behind the arcades of the Place des Vosges, opened in 1612 as the Place Royale — the oldest planned square in Paris.

The house takes its name from Anne of Austria. Victor Hugo and Colette both lived on the square.

Saint-Paul, Chemin Vert and Bastille metro stations are each about four hundred metres away.
