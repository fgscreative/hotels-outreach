The Place · Mane · Haute-Provence

Between Luberon and lavender

The convent stands on the edge of Mane, a hill village in the Alpes-de-Haute-Provence, a few kilometres from Forcalquier.

Founded by Minim friars in the seventeenth century, it is now a hotel, restaurant and spa run with L’Occitane en Provence.

Lavender fields, the Luberon ridge and the market towns of Haute-Provence are all a short drive away.
