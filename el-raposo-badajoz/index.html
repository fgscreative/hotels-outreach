The Place · El Raposo · Zafra, Badajoz

In the south of Badajoz

El Raposo lies in the countryside of southern Badajoz, a few kilometres from Zafra, among the holm-oak pastures and vineyards of Extremadura.

The mud used in the treatments comes from the stream that runs near the spa; few balnearios in Spain work with natural mud this way.

The hotel is joined to the spa building and surrounded by thirty thousand square metres of gardens.
