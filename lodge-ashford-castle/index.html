The Place · Cong · County Mayo

On the shore of Lough Corrib

The Lodge was built in 1865 for the estate manager of Ashford Castle, on a rise above Lough Corrib at the edge of Cong.

The lough is one of Ireland’s largest lakes, scattered with islands and backed by the hills of Connemara to the west.

The castle, its woods and gardens surround the Lodge; Galway is about an hour away by car.
