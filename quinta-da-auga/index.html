The Place · Santiago de Compostela · Galicia

A paper mill on the Sar

A Quinta da Auga occupies an eighteenth-century paper mill on the river Sar, restored and opened as a hotel in 2009.

Its gardens and old trees run down to the water; the old town of Santiago de Compostela and its cathedral are a short drive away.

Granite, rain and green: the Atlantic climate keeps the valley soft all year.
