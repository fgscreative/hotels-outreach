The Place · Hanioti · Kassandra, Halkidiki

In the village of Hanioti

Ampelia stands in a quiet corner of Hanioti, a village on Kassandra, the westernmost of Halkidiki’s three peninsulas.

The beach and the Toronean Gulf are about 800 metres away; along this coast, pine woods run right down to the sand.

Thessaloniki airport is around an hour and a quarter by car.
