The Place · Gare de Lyon · Paris 12e

On Rue de Lyon

Hôtel de France stands at 12 Rue de Lyon in the 12th arrondissement, on a street opened in 1847 between the Place de la Bastille and the new Gare de Lyon.

Rue Crémieux, the Aligre market and Rue de Lappe are a few minutes away; the Coulée Verte walkway runs above the Viaduc des Arts nearby.

The Marais, the Place des Vosges and Bercy are each a short walk or one metro stop.
