The Place · Amsterdam Oost

In Amsterdam Oost

Volkshotel lives in the 1960s headquarters of De Volkskrant on the Wibautstraat, in Amsterdam Oost.

The outside is kept as it was; inside are 85 workspaces for the makers of Broedplaats VKG, and 262 solar panels on the roof.

The Amstel, De Pijp and the Waterlooplein market are all close by.
