The Place · Es Capdellà · Mallorca

Under the Tramuntana

The castle stands above Es Capdellà in the south-west of Mallorca, at the foot of the Serra de Tramuntana, listed by UNESCO in 2011.

Records of the estate go back to 1450; as a working possessió it once housed up to eighty people. It opened as a hotel in 2013.

Palma is about twenty-five minutes away; the Puig de Galatzó, 1,027 m, rises behind the estate.
