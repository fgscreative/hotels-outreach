The Place · Cassago Brianza · Lake Como hills

In the Brianza

C-Hotel stands in Cassago Brianza, in the wooded hills between Milan and Lake Como.

Andrea Colzani, a self-taught architect, built it in 2009 around his family’s pastry shop, in stone and teak.

The garden is by landscape architect Emilio Trabella; the lake is a short drive north.
