The Place · San Marco · Venezia

Opposite the Salute, beside San Marco

Palazzo Dandolo rises straight from the Grand Canal, where it opens into the Bacino di San Marco.

Across the water stands Longhena's Santa Maria della Salute; behind you, St Mark's Square is a two-minute walk.

Inside lies the Ridotto, where Venice opened Europe’s first public gaming house in 1638.
