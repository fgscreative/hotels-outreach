The Place · Sultanahmet · Istanbul

In Sultanahmet

Historia stands in Sultanahmet, the heart of old Istanbul, in one of two neighbouring wooden Ottoman mansions whose residents were close to the Sultan.

The Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia are a short walk away; Topkapı Palace and the Hippodrome a little further.

From the upper floors the Sea of Marmara spreads out below.
