The Place · Costa Teguise · Lanzarote

On the lava coast of Costa Teguise

El Guarapo sits in Costa Teguise, on the sheltered east coast of Lanzarote, where the trade winds take the edge off summer.

Between 1730 and 1736 the Timanfaya eruptions buried a quarter of the island in new lava; in 1993 the whole island became a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve.

César Manrique's Jardín de Cactus is fifteen minutes north.
