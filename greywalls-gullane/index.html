The Place · Gullane · East Lothian

Links, dunes and the Firth of Forth

Greywalls was designed by Edwin Lutyens in 1901 as a holiday house, its walled gardens in the spirit of Gertrude Jekyll.

It looks over Muirfield to the Firth of Forth, about twenty miles east of Edinburgh on the East Lothian coast.

Gullane’s dunes, the Aberlady Bay nature reserve and the gannets of Bass Rock are all close by.
