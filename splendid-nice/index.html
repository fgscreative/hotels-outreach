The Place · Nice · Côte d’Azur

In central Nice

The Splendid stands on Boulevard Victor Hugo in the centre of Nice, a few blocks from the Promenade des Anglais and the beaches.

From the eighth floor the view runs over the roofs to the Baie des Anges and the Colline du Château.

The Tschann family has welcomed travellers here for four generations.
