The Place · Castellammare di Stabia · Bay of Naples

Above the Bay of Naples

La Medusa stands on the Varano hill above Castellammare di Stabia, on the Passeggiata Archeologica, facing the Bay of Naples and Vesuvius.

On the same hill lie the Roman villas of ancient Stabiae, buried by the eruption of AD 79 that covered Pompeii.

Naples and Salerno are about twenty minutes away, Sorrento around twenty-five; boats leave the harbour for Capri and the Amalfi Coast.
