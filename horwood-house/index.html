The Place · Little Horwood · Buckinghamshire

In 38 acres

Horwood House stands in 38 acres of woods, lawns and formal gardens near Little Horwood, in the north Buckinghamshire countryside.

The manor was built in 1911 in the Arts & Crafts style and is Grade II listed; a modern wing stands beside it.

Milton Keynes Central is about ten minutes by car, with trains to London Euston in around half an hour.
