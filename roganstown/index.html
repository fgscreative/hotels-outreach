The Place · Swords · North County Dublin

In Fingal

Roganstown lies off the Naul Road, about 3 km north-west of Swords in north County Dublin.

The house dates from the 1820s; the 1866 map shows parkland, woods and a formal garden, and three generations of McLoughlins farmed here from 1916.

Fingal takes its name from Fine Gall, the land of the foreigners — the Vikings.
