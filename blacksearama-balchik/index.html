The Place · Balchik · Black Sea coast

Above the bay of Balchik

BlackSeaRama sits on the clifftops of Bulgaria’s northern Black Sea coast, a few kilometres east of Balchik.

From two hundred metres up, the view takes in the whole bay, from Cape Kaliakra to Cape Galata near Varna.

Balchik’s palace and botanical garden, built for Queen Marie of Romania, are minutes away; Varna airport is under an hour.
