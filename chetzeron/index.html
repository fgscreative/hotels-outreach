The Place · Crans-Montana · Valais

Above Crans-Montana

Chetzeron sits above Crans-Montana, on the ski area in winter and the alpine pasture in summer, facing the highest summits of the Alps.

Big windows look south across the Rhône valley to the peaks of the Pennine Alps.

Crans-Montana lies below; Sion and the valley floor are about half an hour further down.
