The Place · Palheiro Estate · Madeira

Above Funchal

The lodge stands in the uplands above Funchal on the Palheiro estate, laid out from 1801 and owned by the Blandy family since 1885.

King Carlos stayed here in 1901. Today the estate holds gardens, forest and an 18-hole course.

The endemic Trocaz pigeon and the Macaronesian sparrowhawk nest here; the airport is twenty minutes away.
