The Place · Lindos · Rhodes

Above the bay

Lindos Sun stands on a hillside of rosemary and sage on the east coast of Rhodes, above the bay where The Guns of Navarone was filmed.

Lindos village and its acropolis are a kilometre away; so is St Paul’s Bay. Pefkos is two.

It was the first hotel built in the Lindos area, in 1985.
