The Place · Enniskerry · Wicklow Mountains

Above Enniskerry

Summerhill House stands on the hill above Enniskerry, a village whose Irish name, Áth na Sceire, means the rocky ford.

In the 1840s it belonged to William Richard Le Fanu; his brother Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu, the Gothic novelist, was a regular guest.

Powerscourt, its waterfall and the Sugar Loaf are close; Dublin is thirty minutes away.
