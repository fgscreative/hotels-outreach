The Place · Île de Cavallo · Corsica

Between Corsica and Sardinia

Cavallo is a small granite island in the Strait of Bonifacio, between the south of Corsica and the north of Sardinia.

There are no cars: guests move by bicycle or electric cart, and the hotel is the only one on the island.

Next door lie the Lavezzi islands, part of the Bouches de Bonifacio nature reserve, where the water stays clear to the seabed.
