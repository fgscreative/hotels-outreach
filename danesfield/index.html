The Place · Marlow · Buckinghamshire

Above the Thames

Danesfield House stands on chalk cliffs above a bend in the Thames between Marlow and Henley; a Danish encampment once gave the site its name.

Finished in 1901 for Robert William Hudson, it was requisitioned in 1941 as RAF Medmenham, home of wartime photographic intelligence. It became a hotel in 1991.

Muntjac and fallow deer roam the grounds; London is about thirty minutes away.
