The Place · Lignano Pineta · Friuli

In Lignano Pineta

Hotel Mimosa stands on Corso degli Alisei in Lignano Pineta, among the pines, 400 m from a Blue Flag beach.

Lignano has eight kilometres of fine sand between the mouth of the Tagliamento and the Marano lagoon.

Trieste airport is 60 km away, Venice 85 km.
