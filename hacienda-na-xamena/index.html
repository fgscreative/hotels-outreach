The Place · Na Xamena · Ibiza

In Es Amunts

Hacienda Na Xamena stands on the cliffs above Na Xamena bay, in the Es Amunts protected area on Ibiza’s quiet north-west coast.

Below, meadows of Posidonia seagrass — part of Ibiza’s UNESCO World Heritage listing — keep the water clear.

Sant Miquel is about ten minutes away; Ibiza town around thirty and the airport about thirty-five.
