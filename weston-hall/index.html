The Place · Weston · Staffordshire

In Staffordshire

Weston Hall stands on Weston Bank, a few miles north-east of Stafford, in twenty acres of Staffordshire countryside.

Inside, the Elizabethan hall keeps its open stone fireplaces and oak beams, and the bedrooms look far across the county.

The Shugborough estate is a few minutes away, and the heaths and woods of Cannock Chase a short drive south.
