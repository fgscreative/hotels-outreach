The Place · Hatfield Heath · Essex

On the county border

Down Hall stands near Hatfield Heath, close to Bishop’s Stortford, on the border of Essex and Hertfordshire.

The estate’s history goes back to the 11th century; today’s Italianate mansion dates from the 19th, in 110 acres of gardens and woodland.

Central London is about forty-five minutes away, and Stansted Airport closer still.
