The Place · Hafling · Avelengo · above Merano

A ledge above the Merano basin

Miramonti sits at 1,230 metres in Hafling, on the sunny edge of the Tschögglberg plateau, some 900 metres above Merano.

Larch and pine forests climb behind the house; in front, the land falls away to the spa town and the Adige valley.

Hafling gave its name to the Haflinger, the golden mountain horse still bred on these slopes.
