The Place · Dorridge · Solihull

In the Forest of Arden

Hogarths Solihull stands in its own woodland and grounds on Four Ashes Road in Dorridge, in the old Forest of Arden.

Birmingham Airport and the NEC are about fifteen minutes away; Birmingham city centre about half an hour.

Its sister, Hogarths Stone Manor, sits in rural Worcestershire near Kidderminster, looking over open countryside.
