The Place · Fritton · Norfolk–Suffolk border

The wild east of England

Fritton Lake lies on the Norfolk–Suffolk border, a long, quiet lake fringed by woodland and meadow on the Somerleyton estate.

Around it, more than a thousand acres have been handed back to nature in one of England’s pioneering rewilding projects.

The club is open to cabin owners, members and holiday guests; the coast at Great Yarmouth is a short drive away.
