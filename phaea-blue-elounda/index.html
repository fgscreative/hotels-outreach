The Place · Plaka · Elounda, Crete

Facing Spinalonga

Phāea Blue sits on the beach at Plaka, a fishing village on the Gulf of Elounda in north-eastern Crete.

Across a narrow strait lies Spinalonga, the Venetian fortress island; boats cross from Plaka in minutes.

Agios Nikolaos is about twenty minutes away, Heraklion airport around an hour by car.
