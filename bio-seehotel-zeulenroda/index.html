The Place · Zeulenrodaer Meer · Thuringia

On the Zeulenrodaer Meer

The hotel stands on the shore of the Zeulenrodaer Meer, a reservoir in the rolling hills of the Thuringian Vogtland.

Beech and spruce woods run down to the water; paths and cycle routes follow the shore.

Leipzig is about an hour and a half by car.
