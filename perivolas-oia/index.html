The Place · Oia · Santorini

On the rim of the caldera

Perivolas stands on the caldera cliffs just outside Oia, at the northern tip of Santorini.

The houses were once cave dwellings, dug into the volcanic rock some three hundred years ago and restored by the family that still runs them.

Below lies the sea-filled caldera left by the Bronze Age eruption; Thirasia sits across the water to the west.
