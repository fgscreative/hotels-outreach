The Place · Santa Eulària · Ibiza

Beside Cala Pada

The hotel stands among pines on the s’Argamassa coast, just north-east of Santa Eulària des Riu, on Ibiza’s quieter east side.

Cala Pada’s sand is a three-minute walk; Es Canar and its Wednesday market at Punta Arabí are a short drive up the coast.

Ibiza Town is about twenty minutes by car, the airport about twenty-five.
