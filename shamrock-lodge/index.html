The Place · Athlone · River Shannon

On the Shannon

Athlone sits at the heart of Ireland where the Shannon leaves Lough Ree. The house stands on the west bank, in its own gardens.

It was built in 1814 for Michael Monks and became a hotel in 1951. Princess Grace visited in 1961.

Athlone Castle is a ten-minute walk; Dublin is 130 km east and Galway 95 km west.
