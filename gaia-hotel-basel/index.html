The Place · Basel SBB · Switzerland

At Basel SBB

GAIA Hotel stands on Centralbahnstrasse, directly opposite Basel SBB, where the Swiss and French railways meet.

The old town and the Rhine are about fifteen minutes on foot; the Kunstmuseum is ten, the Fondation Beyeler a tram ride away.

EuroAirport is about twenty minutes by bus; Zurich under an hour by train.
