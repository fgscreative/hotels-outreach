The Place · Hrvatsko Zagorje · Croatia

In the green heart of Zagorje

Tuhelj lies in Hrvatsko Zagorje, a quilt of beech woods, vineyards and hilltop chapels about forty minutes north of Zagreb.

Thermal water has risen here for centuries, reaching the surface warm and rich in minerals.

Within an hour stand the castles of Veliki Tabor and Trakošćan — and the green hills simply keep going.
