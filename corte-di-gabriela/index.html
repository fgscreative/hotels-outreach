The Place · San Marco · Venice

Between Rialto and San Marco

Corte di Gabriela is a restored nineteenth-century palazzo on Calle degli Avvocati, in the San Marco sestiere.

The Grand Canal, Rialto, Palazzo Grassi and La Fenice are all a short walk, through alleys, over bridges and past the markets.

Inside are eleven rooms, suites and an apartment, around a private courtyard where breakfast is served.
