The Place · Beaulieu · New Forest

In the New Forest

The Montagu Arms stands at the head of the Beaulieu River, where the tide meets the heath and ancient woodland of the New Forest.

An inn stood here in the 16th century, first The Ship, then The George, host to the Beaulieu fairs from 1607. The front was rebuilt in Arts & Crafts style in 1887–88.

The National Motor Museum and Exbury Gardens are close by.
