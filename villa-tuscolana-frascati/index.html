The Place · Frascati · Castelli Romani

Above Frascati

Villa Tuscolana — La Rufinella — is the highest of the Tusculan villas above Frascati, in the Alban Hills south-east of Rome.

It was built in 1578 for Cardinal Alessandro Rufini on the ruins of Cicero’s villa, and reshaped by Luigi Vanvitelli for the Jesuits around 1700.

Lucien Bonaparte lived here and Victor Emmanuel II paid for excavations in 1872; Castel Gandolfo and Lake Albano are close.
