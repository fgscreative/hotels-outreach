The Place · Newbury · Berkshire

In West Berkshire

Donnington sits in 120 acres of rolling parkland on the Old Oxford Road, just north of Newbury.

The course runs to 6,221 yards from the back tees, with water on fifteen, sixteen and seventeen.

The ruins of Donnington Castle stand nearby; the North Wessex Downs and the chalk stream of the Lambourn lie beyond.
