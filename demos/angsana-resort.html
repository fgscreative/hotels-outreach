The Place

One degree north
of the equator.

The Maldivian atoll sits at the precise latitude where the Indian Ocean turns luminous — where water temperature holds at 28°C year-round and visibility drops to 30 metres below the keel.

This is not an island. It is a living reef system with a runway attached — 1,200 species of marine life within swimming distance of your villa steps.

Here, the concept of a spa extends to every surface the ocean touches. Healing is not a programme. It is geography.
