The Place

The Strait
at Its Narrowest
and Most Human

Üsküdar sits on the Asian shore where the Bosphorus bends and slows. The water here is narrow enough to read the ferry schedules of the European side. The distance between civilisations: nine minutes.

Harem Hotel faces that crossing. From the terrace, the Maiden's Tower rises from the water exactly as it has for a thousand years — impervious to the container ships that pass before it each morning.

Istanbul does not ask you to choose between continents. It asks you to hold both, to stand in the space between, and to breathe there.
