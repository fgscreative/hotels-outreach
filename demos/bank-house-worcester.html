The Place

Bransford,
between hill and river

Set in the folds of Worcestershire farmland, the Bank House sits where the Malvern Hills break the horizon and the River Teme bends south toward the Wye. The land here has its own particular quality — dense, damp, ancient in the way that English countryside can be.

Six kilometres from Worcester's cathedral tower. Far enough from city rhythm to feel genuinely remote. The grounds give onto working orchards, a parkland golf course and the spa's sculpted gardens with their sightline to the hills.

Summer brings the smell of cut grass and warm stone. Winter brings mist along the valley floor and fires lit by early afternoon. Both are reasons enough.
