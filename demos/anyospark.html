The Place

At the centre
of the Pyrenees.
Close to everything.

La Massana sits inside a glacial valley carved by the Valira del Nord over 15,000 years. AnyósPark occupies its exact midpoint — neither summit nor valley floor — at the elevation where the air begins to change what you notice.

Five minutes from two world-class ski areas. Equidistant from Caldea thermal spa and Andorra la Vella's duty-free centre. Coma Pedrosa's 2,942-metre face visible from every north-facing window.

The Pyrenees are not backdrop here. They are the architecture. Every view is a reminder that this specific altitude, this specific longitude, cannot be replicated.
