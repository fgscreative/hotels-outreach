The Place

Where the Cyclades
breathe slowly.

Andros is the island the Athenians kept for themselves. Lush valleys cut between stone ridges. Wild beaches where the current runs cold and clear. The air carries oregano and salt — nothing artificial, nothing performed.

Heliades sits at 180 metres, amphitheatric, watching the sea. The hill protects you from the meltemi. The light comes in horizontal at dusk and turns everything amber. Five minutes down, Fellos beach. Ten minutes north, the port of Gavrio.

This is not a resort. It is four private homes that happen to share the same view. You will not feel managed here. You will feel found.
