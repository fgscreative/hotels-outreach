Sixty-three degrees north of the equator, the light behaves differently. Summer evenings hold colour until midnight; winter afternoons burn gold by three o'clock. Every Best Western property in Scandinavia is positioned within this particular quality of northern light.

The terrain is the amenity. Boreal forest stretches beyond every city limit. Granite archipelagos fracture the coastline into ten thousand islands. Lakes hold water so clear you can see eight metres to the bottom. You don't arrive at Scandinavia. You enter it.
