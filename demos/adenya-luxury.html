Where the mountains
hold the sea

Alanya's eastern littoral carries a particular quality — sheltered from prevailing northerly winds by the Taurus range, this stretch of coastline holds warmth a full month longer than the western Aegean. The swimming season begins in April and rarely ends before November.

Below the surface: Posidonia oceanica meadows, centuries-old, filter the sea to a clarity that registers as depth rather than distance. Above the tideline: limestone terraces where endemic Anatolian orchids bloom through April. The ecology here is not backdrop — it is the material from which the experience is made.

ADENYA sits at that exact intersection — mountain behind, open sea ahead — where nature provides the architecture and the resort adds only what the coastline cannot do alone.
