Adriatic Riviera, Italy

The most enclosed
sea in Europe.

The Adriatic is not the open ocean. It is a basin — mineral-dense, warm in summer, held between two coastlines that have traded salt, wine and silence for three thousand years.

Hotel Atlantic sits at the water's edge, its architecture aligned to the morning light that arrives from the east unobstructed. The sea is not scenery here. It is the building material of every treatment.

Three metres above the tideline. Direct access to the beach at low season, when the Riviera belongs entirely to those who know it well.
