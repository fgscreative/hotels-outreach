The Place

Heart of
North Hampshire.

Basingstoke sits at the northern edge of the chalk downs — where the river Test begins and the air carries flint. Apollo stands at the Aldermaston Roundabout, the axis of everything arriving and departing this corner of southern England.

London Waterloo is under an hour by rail. The New Forest trails begin thirty minutes south. Winchester Cathedral fifteen minutes west. This is not a hotel that asks you to choose between the country and the city — it gives you both.

Independent since its founding. Managed by people who live here, shop here, and walk the same Saturday market as their guests. That proximity translates — you feel it the moment you check in.
