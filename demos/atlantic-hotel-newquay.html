Newquay · Cornwall

Where Cornwall meets
open water

Newquay's headland drops sharply to the sea. The Atlantic Hotel has occupied this edge since 1892 — not facing the ocean as an amenity, but as an orientation. Building and coastline have become inseparable.

The rugged North Cornwall cliff face forms a 60-metre shelf below the spa level. In autumn, the swells arrive from Iceland and build across three thousand miles of unbroken Atlantic. In summer, the harbour fills with colour and Fistral Beach draws the world's best surfers.

Fifty-seven rooms. Each one angled toward the sea. Because here, the view is not incidental — it is the entire point.
