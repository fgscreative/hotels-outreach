The Place

Where Norfolk breathes.

Fourteen metres above the River Yare. The land here is ancient — chalk under clay, oak above meadow, the river threading south towards Norwich at its own unhurried speed. This is not dramatic terrain. It is quiet, specific, and profoundly itself.

The two golf courses read the natural contours of the valley floor: the Valley course follows the lower ground, tight between stands of oak; the River course opens into longer, wind-exposed fairways where Norfolk sky becomes the dominant element.

Nine miles west of Norwich, four miles from Wymondham. Far enough from everything. Close enough to everything that matters.

Ecological data: River Yare floodplain, Site of Special Scientific Interest. Common reed Phragmites australis, Grey heron Ardea cinerea, Barn owl Tyto alba. Last ice: 12,000 BP. Current elevation gain from sea: 14m.

Spa & Wellness

Six rituals. One intention.

Dawn Float

Thermal pool. First light across the valley. Forty-five minutes that change the rest of the day.

Norfolk Clay Wrap

Mineral-dense earth from the valley. Applied warm, left to work, washed away. What it draws out surprises people.

Riverside Restore

Gentle movement on the Yare bank. Slow stretching, river sound, the particular quiet of moving water beside you.

Hammam Ritual

Heat chamber, black soap, exfoliation to the grain. Three stages of warmth, ending in cold and silence.

Sound Bath

Himalayan bowls, garden dusk, the valley as amplifier. Sixty minutes that recalibrate what you thought was silence.

Nordic Circuit

Sauna, cold plunge, rest. Repeated. The Norwegian sequence. Your cardiovascular system remembers it for days.
