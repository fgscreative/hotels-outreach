Greetham · Rutland · England

England's smallest county.
Its most considered golf.

Rutland sits on a plateau of limestone and clay between the Welland and Gwash valleys. The glacial drift that built the terrain here also shaped the two championship fairways that run through it — one reading north, one east into the prevailing wind.

The village of Greetham stands at 82 metres above sea level. It holds two medieval churches, one pub, and the golf club that has been working the same greens since the late twentieth century. Rutland Water is nine miles west. Stamford is twelve east.

Three courses, a Toptracer range, a hotel and eight self-catering lodges — arranged without urgency across the valley floor. The landscape does more than frame the game here. It sets the conditions.
