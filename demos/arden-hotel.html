The Place

Ancient woodland,
five minutes from the airport.

The Forest of Arden — where Shakespeare set his comedies of transformation — lies in the folds of north Warwickshire. Oak, ash, field maple. The light here changes by the hour.

We sit at 105 metres elevation, close enough to the NEC to be convenient, far enough from anywhere to feel removed. That paradox is exactly the point.

Step outside and the air holds wild garlic in spring, woodsmoke in autumn. The seasons still arrive here. We keep a note of them.
