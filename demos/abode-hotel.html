Inside the city walls. Eight metres above the Stour.

Canterbury has been a place of arrival for twelve centuries. Pilgrims, merchants, travellers drawn by the spire. Now you arrive on the High Street, steps from the Cathedral Quarter, and the city opens at walking pace.

Chester sits differently — Roman walls ringing a city that still runs a racecourse, still watches the Dee move through its meadows. Both places reward the guest who slows down.

ABode holds you in the heart of each. No shuttle required.
