The Place

Two islands.
One sea.

Ibiza sits at 38.9°N in the Balearic Sea — a limestone ridge rising 475 metres from water that turns transparent in July. The island has been inhabited for 2,700 years. The sea has not changed colour.

Twenty minutes by ferry, Mallorca opens wider — broader bays, older pines, markets with saffron and cured meats. The same light, refracted differently.

Both islands share a geological shelf and a particular quality of morning. At azuLine, you belong to both.
