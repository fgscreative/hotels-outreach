The Place

Pamplona.
Old enough to have
earned its silence.

Pamplona is the capital of Navarra — a kingdom before it was a region, a city before most European capitals existed. Its Roman walls still hold the old town together. Its bullring is a cathedral of another kind.

At 449m above sea level, the city sits at the Pyrenean foothills — close enough to France to feel it in the architecture, close enough to the Ebro valley to feel it in the wine. The Arga river curves through the city like a sentence with an open ending.

BED4U is not a postcard. It is an insider's key — to the local market, the morning run along the walls, the afternoon pintxo bar that no guidebook has found yet.
