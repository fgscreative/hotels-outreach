Belek · Antalya · Turkish Riviera

A coast that counts
by season, not clock

Belek occupies a particular triangle of the Eastern Mediterranean — where the Taurus range descends into the Antalya plain, where Pinus brutia forest thickens to shoreline, and where the sea runs flat and the light arrives without announcement. That clarity is not metaphor.

Forty kilometres east, the Roman theatre at Aspendos has carried sound across eighteen centuries without amplification. The same limestone that built those seats lines the seabed here. History is not something you visit from Belek. It is the substrate you stand on.

320 days of sun per year. The Taurus peaks hold snow in April while the sea below runs at 22°C. That tension between altitude and warmth is not incidental — it is why this coast breathes differently.
