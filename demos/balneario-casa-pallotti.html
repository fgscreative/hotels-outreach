The Place

Fifty kilometres from Bilbao.
A different pace entirely.

The Valle de Carranza opens into a green corridor where the Río Mayor runs cold and clear between limestone bluffs. The Bosobrón hillside holds the spring that has fed this balneario since before the word wellness existed.

The water surfaces at a constant 30°C — chlorosodic, bicarbonated, mildly mineralised — classified as anti-inflammatory and sedative by the chemists of 1798. It has not changed. You may.

Casa Pallotti was built in 1847 around this spring. The building carries its age deliberately — stone floors that hold the mountain cold, windows that frame the mountain green, rooms that exist on the scale of rest.
