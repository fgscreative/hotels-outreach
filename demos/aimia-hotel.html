Inside
the circular bay

Port de Sóller sits inside one of Majorca's rarest formations — a near-circular bay, open only to the south, sheltered on all other sides by the Serra de Tramuntana. The mountains hold the warmth. The bay holds the light. The swimming season begins in May and rarely ends before October.

Below the surface: Posidonia oceanica meadows on Tethys limestone — an ancient seabed lifted metres above what it once was. The clarity registers as depth. Above the tideline: ochre rock, endemic Majorcan orchids through April, lemon groves that have fruited since the Moorish period. The ecology is not decoration.

The Aimia Hotel is forty metres from the beach. The Serra begins three minutes on foot. The tramway has connected the port to the old town of Sóller through the orange valley since 1912. Arrive. The place already knows what it is.
