The Place

A Mediterranean
Terroir Unlike
Any Other

Finestrat sits at 120 metres above the Bay of Benidorm, shielded from Atlantic wind by the Sierra de Aitana. This accident of geology creates a microclimate tropical enough to sustain bamboo groves, frangipani, and a specimen of every major Southeast Asian species — thriving on Spanish soil.

The same sun that cures the Mediterranean coast pours into the hotel's nine pools from first light. The same warm air that lifts the scent of jasmine and ylang-ylang through the gardens carries it into every open room, every corridor, every breath.

This is not a recreation. It is a genuine convergence — two landscapes that, by some quiet miracle of climate and intention, found each other on the Costa Blanca.
