Golden Sands · Bulgaria

The Black Sea tells
different stories from here.

Golden Sands (Zlatni Pyasaci) sits on the northern arc of the Varna bay, where the Balkan range meets the Black Sea in limestone and sand. The water here is warm from late May. The coast is oldest on the Romanian side and youngest at the resort edge.

The GRIFID complex occupies a direct shoreline position — eight properties, each facing the same sea from a different angle. The Foresta hotel sits inside a preserved broadleaf forest behind the beach. The Noa 5★ sits on the water's edge. Both face Bulgaria's longest natural beach.

Thirty kilometres separates the complex from Varna airport. The transfer is complimentary from six nights. The sea is thirty metres from the cheapest room in the collection.
