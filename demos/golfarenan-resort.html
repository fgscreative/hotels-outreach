The Place

Halmstad.
Where the Nissan
meets the open sea.

The Golfarena sits on Sweden's west coast, in Halland county, where heathland, pine forest, and the Kattegat converge in a single uninterrupted horizon. On clear days, you can see Denmark from the high point of hole seven.

Walking distance: a fine sandy beach. Cycling distance: several nature reserves. Within reach: Tylösand, Halmstad's celebrated coastal headland. The city center is a twelve-minute bus ride — irrelevant to most guests.

This is a working landscape. The light changes hourly. The wind is a playing partner, not an inconvenience. You don't visit Halmstad. You calibrate to it.
