The Place · Chamonix · Mont-Blanc

Under the Aiguille du Midi

Le Morgane stands in the centre of Chamonix, at 1,035 m on the valley floor, a hundred metres from the Aiguille du Midi cable car.

The cable car climbs to 3,842 m, facing Mont Blanc at 4,806 m; Le Brévent rises on the other side of the valley.

Geneva airport is about an hour away by road.
