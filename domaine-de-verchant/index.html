The Place · Castelnau-le-Lez · Montpellier

At Montpellier’s edge

Domaine de Verchant lies at Castelnau-le-Lez, ten minutes from the centre of Montpellier, on the rolled-pebble terroir of La Méjanelle.

Pierre Verchant bought the farm in 1582. The Leenhardt family planted resistant vines after phylloxera and had the park laid out; it is now a listed monument.

The Mestres bought it in 2002, restored it for seven years and opened in 2007.
