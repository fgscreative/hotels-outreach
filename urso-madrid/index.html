The Place · Chamberí · Madrid

A neoclassical palace in Chamberí

URSO occupies a neoclassical palace from 1915 on Calle de Mejía Lequerica, between Alonso Martínez and the Salesas quarter.

Its marble halls and high ceilings were restored as 78 rooms and suites, with a spa at the heart of the building.

Gran Vía and the Paseo de Recoletos are a short walk; the Prado is under half an hour on foot.
