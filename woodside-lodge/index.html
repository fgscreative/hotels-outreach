The Place · Lochgoilhead · Argyll

On a Highland sea loch

Woodside Lodge stands at the head of Loch Goil, a sea loch running off Loch Long on the west coast of Scotland.

The estate is twelve acres of mature woodland and open ground, inside Loch Lomond & The Trossachs National Park.

Glasgow is about an hour and a half by road; the Rest and Be Thankful pass is on the way.
