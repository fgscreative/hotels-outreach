The Place · Singel · Amsterdam

On the Singel, since the 1600s

Hotel Estheréa occupies 17th-century buildings at Singel 303–309, on the oldest of Amsterdam’s main canals.

The Canal Ring has been on the UNESCO World Heritage list since 2010; the Dam, the Anne Frank House and the Jordaan are all on foot.

What began as a small guest house has been run by the same family for more than eighty years.
