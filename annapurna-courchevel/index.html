The Place · Courchevel 1850 · Les 3 Vallées

Above the altiport

The Annapurna stands on the Route de l’Altiport at the top of Courchevel 1850, under the Saulire, the highest hotel in the resort.

The family named it after the Himalayan peak climbed in 1950 by their friends Maurice Herzog and Louis Lachenal — and so that it came first in the directories.

Les 3 Vallées’ 600 km of slopes start at the door.
