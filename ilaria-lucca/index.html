The Place · Centro storico · Lucca

A canal street inside the walls

Via del Fosso follows the old canal through the quiet eastern quarter of Lucca's historic centre.

Around it, the city's Renaissance walls run unbroken for 4.2 km — never used in battle, now a park in the sky.

Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, the cathedral and Ilaria del Carretto's marble tomb are all a short walk away.
