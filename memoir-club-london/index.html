The Place · Bloomsbury · London

In the squares of Bloomsbury

The Memoir Club sits on Upper Woburn Place, between Euston and Russell Square, on the edge of Tavistock Square.

Virginia Woolf lived on Tavistock Square from 1924; Keynes and the Bells were round the corner on Gordon Square.

The British Museum, King’s Cross and St Pancras are all within an easy walk.
