The Place · Stobo · Scottish Borders

In the Borders

Stobo Castle stands on a wooded slope of the upper Tweed valley, seven miles from Peebles, with the Borders hills on every side.

It was built between 1805 and 1811 by Archibald and James Elliot. The Japanese Water Gardens were laid out in the early 1900s; their Katsura trees smell of burnt sugar in autumn.

Dawyck Botanic Garden is nearby; Edinburgh is about twenty-five miles north.
