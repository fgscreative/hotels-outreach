The Place · Santa Maria Novella · Florence

On Santa Maria Novella

The hotel faces the marble façade of Santa Maria Novella, completed by Leon Battista Alberti in 1470.

The Duomo is about seven minutes on foot and the Ponte Vecchio about twelve; Santa Maria Novella station is around the corner.

The palazzo has welcomed travellers for two centuries, and was reshaped by Carlo Scarpa in 1957.
