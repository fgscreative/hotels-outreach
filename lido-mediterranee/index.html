The Place · Spisone · Taormina Mare

Below Taormina

The Lido Méditerranée stands on the beach at Spisone, in Taormina Mare, below the cliffs of Taormina.

The cable car climbs to the town and Corso Umberto in three minutes; Isola Bella is 1.5 km along the shore.

Etna rises to the south-west, and the Ancient Theatre looks out over the sea towards it.
