The Place · Costa Adeje · Tenerife

In La Caleta

Villa María sits on the slope above La Caleta, a fishing village at the northern end of Costa Adeje, with Golf Costa Adeje on one side and the Atlantic on the other.

La Gomera rises across the channel to the west; inland, Mount Teide — at 3,715 m the highest peak in Spain — crowns a national park of lava fields.

Tenerife South airport is about twenty minutes by car.
