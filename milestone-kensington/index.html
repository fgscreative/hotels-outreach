The Place · Kensington Court · London

Opposite Kensington Palace

The Milestone faces Kensington Gardens from the corner of Kensington Court, in two late-Victorian mansions of red brick and carved stone.

Across the road lie Kensington Palace, the Round Pond and the Italian Gardens; the Royal Albert Hall and the South Kensington museums are a short walk.

Inside, the Tollman family keeps a house of antiques, original art and rooms no two alike.
