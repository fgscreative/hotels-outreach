The Place · Giulianova · Adriatic coast

On the Lungomare

Hotel Cristallo stands on Lungomare Zara in Giulianova Lido, a hundred metres from the Adriatic.

The beach is wide and sandy, lined with pines and palms; the old town sits on the hill above, a kilometre away.

The Casaccia family has run the hotel for generations and keeps it open all year.
