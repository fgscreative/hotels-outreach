The Place · Bad Gögging · Hallertau

On the Abens

Der Eisvogel stands on the Abens at the edge of Bad Gögging, between the Danube wetlands and the hop gardens of the Hallertau, halfway between Munich and Nuremberg.

It began in 1949 as a three-table café in a converted field barn. Thermal water was struck in Bad Gögging in 1975; the hotel opened at Easter 1989.

The Altmühltal lies just to the north.
