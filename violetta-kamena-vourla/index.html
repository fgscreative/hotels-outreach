The Place · Kamena Vourla · Maliakos Gulf

On the Maliakos

Hotel Violetta stands on the beach promenade of Kamena Vourla, facing the North Euboean Gulf, an hour and a half from Athens.

The town’s thermal springs rise at the foot of Mt Knimis at 35 to 36 °C; the Thermopylae springs are a short drive away.

From Agios Konstantinos, ferries cross to Skiathos and Skopelos.
