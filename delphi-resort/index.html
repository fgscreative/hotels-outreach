The Place · Leenane · Connemara

In the Delphi Valley

Delphi lies in a glacial valley on the Galway–Mayo border, with forest and bog on the floor and bare mountains on either side.

At its mouth is Killary Harbour, Ireland’s fjord, sixteen kilometres long; across it rises Mweelrea, the highest peak in Connacht.

Leenane is ten minutes away, Westport thirty and Galway about eighty.
