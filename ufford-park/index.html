The Place · Woodbridge · Suffolk Coast

Near the Deben

Ufford Park lies in 120 acres of parkland at Melton, just outside Woodbridge, where the River Deben widens towards the sea.

Up the river is Sutton Hoo, the Anglo-Saxon ship burial. Woodbridge’s Tide Mill has stood on the quay since the 12th century.

Rendlesham Forest, Orford Ness and its grey seals, Aldeburgh and Snape Maltings are all within half an hour.
