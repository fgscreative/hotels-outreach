The Place · Villa Borghese · Roma

On the Pincian hill

Hotel Splendide Royal stands on Via di Porta Pinciana, between Via Veneto and the Aurelian Walls, facing the gardens of Villa Borghese.

The Spanish Steps are about ten minutes downhill on foot; the Galleria Borghese a walk through the park.

Termini station is about fifteen minutes by taxi; Fiumicino around forty-five.
