The Place · Inishowen · Co. Donegal

On the shore of Lough Foyle

Redcastle stands on the eastern edge of the Inishowen Peninsula, its lawns running down to the tidal waters of Lough Foyle.

Across the lough rise the hills of Derry; just up the road, the Wild Atlantic Way begins its 2,500 km run down Ireland's west coast.

Mature parkland and woodland wrap the house, with a nine-hole course laid along the water.
