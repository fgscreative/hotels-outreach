The Place · Moissac · River Tarn

On the Tarn

The Moulin stands on the bank of the Tarn at Moissac, facing the Île de Beaucaire nature reserve.

The Viguier de Ricard brothers built it in 1474; with thirty-six millstones it became the largest mill in the south-west. It burned in 1916 and became a hotel in 1932, when seaplanes landed on the river.

During the war, Shatta and Bouli Simon sheltered five hundred Jewish children here.
