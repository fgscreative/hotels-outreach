The Place · Fiesole hills · Florence

On the Fiesole hill

Il Salviatino stands on the lower slope of the Fiesole hill, its five hectares of garden looking down on the Duomo.

The Bardi family rebuilt a farmhouse here in the 15th century; Alamanno Salviati added the frescoes, and Ugo Ojetti’s salon brought Dalí and D’Annunzio.

The Rovati family restored it; Alessandra Rovati Vitali, owner and creative director, designed the spa.
