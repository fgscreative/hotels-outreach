The Place · Getå · Bråviken

On Bråviken

Villa Fridhem stands at Getå on the north shore of Bråviken, the long bay that runs in from the Baltic to Norrköping.

Behind it rises the Kolmården forest; Kolmården Wildlife Park is a few minutes along the shore.

Norrköping is about twenty-five minutes away, with a free shuttle from the hotel; Stockholm around two hours.
