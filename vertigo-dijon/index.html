The Place · Place Darcy · Dijon

Place Darcy, Dijon

Vertigo faces Place Darcy, at the gate to Dijon's pedestrian centre and its streets of half-timber and glazed tile roofs.

The city is the northern door to the Climats of Burgundy — 1,247 vineyard plots listed by UNESCO in 2015.

Touch the stone owl on Notre-Dame for luck, then follow the Route des Grands Crus 60 km south.
