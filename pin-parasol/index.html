The Place · Lac du Jaunay · Vendée

Above the Lac du Jaunay

Le Pin Parasol sits among pines in the green bocage of the Vendée, fifty metres from the Lac du Jaunay.

The beaches of the Atlantic coast are fifteen minutes away, and the Puy du Fou is within reach for a day.

Monique and Guy Martineau welcomed the first guests in July 1994; their daughter Céline and Patrice run it today.
