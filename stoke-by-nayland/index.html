The Place · Leavenheath · Dedham Vale

In Constable Country

Stoke by Nayland sits on the Essex–Suffolk border in the Dedham Vale, the valley of the Stour that Constable and Gainsborough painted.

Bill and Devora Peake began with 120 acres of apples in 1938. Copella was created in Devora’s farmhouse kitchen in 1969; the name joins Cox’s Orange Pippin and their daughter Carmella.

The courses joined the Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty in 1989.
