The Place · Binz · Rügen

On the Prorer Wiek

The Grand Hotel stands on the beach promenade of Binz, the white resort town on the Prorer Wiek bay of Rügen, Germany’s largest island.

Behind it rises the Granitz beech forest, with its hunting lodge on the hill. The chalk cliffs of Jasmund are about twenty kilometres north.

Every room has a balcony or a terrace, to the sea or to the forest.
