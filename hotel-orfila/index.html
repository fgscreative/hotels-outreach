The Place · Chamberí · Madrid

In Chamberí

Orfila stands on a quiet street in Chamberí, the residential quarter of Madrid’s 19th-century expansion, a block from the Paseo de la Castellana.

The Sorolla Museum is a short walk; the Prado, Thyssen and Reina Sofía — the Paseo del Arte — are about five minutes by car, the Retiro a little further.

Atocha station is about ten minutes away; Madrid-Barajas airport around twenty.
