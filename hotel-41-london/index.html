The Place · Buckingham Palace Road · London

Across the road from the Palace

Hotel 41 occupies the upper floors of a corner building on Buckingham Palace Road, looking over the Royal Mews.

St James's Park, Green Park and the Mall are a short walk; Victoria station is around the corner.

Above the traffic, the hotel keeps the hush of a private house — thirty rooms, one lounge, a team that learns your name.
