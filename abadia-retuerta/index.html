The Place · Sardón de Duero · Valladolid

In the Duero valley

Abadía Retuerta stands on the south bank of the Duero at Sardón de Duero, in the Ribera del Duero wine country east of Valladolid.

The estate covers about 700 hectares around a Romanesque abbey founded in the twelfth century, with eight centuries of documented winemaking.

Valladolid is about twenty-five minutes by car; Madrid less than two hours.
