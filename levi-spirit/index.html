The Place · Levi · Finnish Lapland

Beside the Ounasjoki

Levi Spirit lies on the quieter east side of Levi fell, among pines along the Ounasjoki river in Finnish Lapland.

Levi has 43 slopes and 27 lifts from October to the end of May, more than 200 km of ski trails and 900 km of snowmobile routes.

Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park is close by; Kittilä airport is fifteen minutes away.

  • Kittilä airport15 min
  • Levi slopes43
  • Ski trails200+ km
