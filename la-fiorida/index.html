The Place · Mantello · Valtellina

In Valtellina

La Fiorida lies on the floor of Valtellina at Mantello, between the Rhaetian Alps to the north and the Orobic Alps to the south, near the top of Lake Como.

Plinio Vanini started the farm in 1983; today it keeps 230 Brown Swiss cows in a smart barn, about 100 goats, and farms around 100 hectares.

In 2013 La Preséf became the first agriturismo restaurant in Italy to win a Michelin star; a Green Star followed in 2022.
