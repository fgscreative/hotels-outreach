The Place · Taylor’s Hill · Galway

On Taylor’s Hill

The Ardilaun stands in its own gardens on Taylor’s Hill, a leafy rise between Galway city and Salthill.

A country mansion before it was a hotel, it has been run by the Ryan family for over sixty years and has hosted weddings since 1962.

The city is a kilometre away; Connemara and the Aran Islands start just west.
