The Place · Torri del Benaco · Lake Garda

On the olive riviera

Al Caminetto stands on the Gardesana in Torri del Benaco, on the eastern shore of Lake Garda — the Riviera degli Ulivi.

The Consolini family has kept the hotel for more than fifty years and rebuilt it as a house of suites for adults.

Torri’s Scaliger castle and harbour are a short walk; Monte Baldo rises behind.
