The Place · Dosrius · Maresme

In the Montnegre i el Corredor

The cabins stand above Canyamars in the Maresme hills, in a forest of pine, holm oak and cork oak between the sea and the mountains.

Paths lead to the Santuari del Corredor at 657 m, the Dolmen de Ca l’Arenes and the 18 m Can Ferrerons cork oak, listed as a monumental tree.

Barcelona is about forty minutes away.
