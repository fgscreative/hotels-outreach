The Place · Forte dei Marmi · Versilia

On the Versilia

Principe Forte dei Marmi stands on Viale Ammiraglio Morin, a block back from the beach at Forte dei Marmi, on the Versilia coast of Tuscany.

Behind the town rise the Apuan Alps, whose Carrara marble once shipped from the old pier — the fort and the marble gave the town its name.

Pisa airport and Lucca are each about forty minutes by road; Florence about an hour and a quarter.
