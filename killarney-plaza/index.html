The Place · Killarney · Co. Kerry

At the park gates

The Plaza stands on the corner of Killarney’s main street, with the gardens of Killarney House directly across the road.

Beyond them lies Killarney National Park — Ireland’s first, founded in 1932 — with Lough Leane, Torc Waterfall, Muckross House, Ross Castle and a native herd of red deer.

The Ring of Kerry and the Gap of Dunloe start from the town.
