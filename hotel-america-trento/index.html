The Place · Trento · Adige Valley

In old Trento

Hotel America stands on Via Torre Verde in the old centre of Trento, a minute’s walk from the Castello del Buonconsiglio.

Domenico Giovannini mined in Rock Springs, Wyoming, from 1910, came home and in 1923 bought the nuns’ Anaunia inn — and named it America.

Bombed on the last day of 1944, it reopened in 1951; the family renovated more than sixty rooms in 2023 without closing.
