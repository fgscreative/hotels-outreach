The Place · Ouranoupoli · Mount Athos

At Ouranoupoli

Eagles Palace lies on a pine-fringed beach at Ouranoupoli, the last village before the monastic state of Mount Athos on Halkidiki’s third peninsula.

Boats leave the village for the Athos coast; the Byzantine tower stands by the harbour, and the Drenia islets and Ammouliani lie offshore.

The family’s hospitality here goes back to 1925.
