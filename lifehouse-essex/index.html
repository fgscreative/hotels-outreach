The Place · Thorpe-le-Soken · Essex

In the gardens of Thorpe Hall

Lifehouse sits in the grounds of Thorpe Hall at Thorpe-le-Soken, its gardens listed by English Heritage.

The Essex coast at Frinton and Walton is a few miles east; London Liverpool Street is about ninety minutes by train.

Inside: clipped hedges, old stone steps and lawns for walking between treatments.
