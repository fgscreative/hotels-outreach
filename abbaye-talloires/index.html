The Place · Talloires · Lake Annecy

On Lake Annecy

The Abbaye stands on the Bay of Talloires, sheltered by the Roc de Chère nature reserve and facing the Château de Duingt across the water.

Queen Theutberga founded a cell here around 866; it became a royal abbey in 1674 and, in 1862, the first hotel-restaurant on the lake.

Cézanne painted here, and Mark Twain stayed in 1891.
