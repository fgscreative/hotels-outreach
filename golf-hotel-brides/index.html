The Place · Brides-les-Bains · 3 Vallées

In the Doron valley

The Golf Hôtel stands above Brides-les-Bains, a spa village in the Doron valley of the Tarentaise, with the Vanoise behind.

The town has taken the waters for some 170 years and receives about twelve thousand spa guests a year.

From the village, the Olympe gondola climbs to Méribel and the 600 km of the 3 Vallées.
