The Place · Ferrycarrig · Wexford

On the Slaney

Ferrycarrig stands on the north bank of the Slaney estuary, four kilometres upriver from Wexford town.

Across the water rise the Norman tower of Ferrycarrig Castle and the Irish National Heritage Park.

Downriver, Greenland white-fronted geese winter on the Wexford Slobs; Curracloe beach is a short drive.
