In Excideuil

The Fin Chapon stands on the château square of Excideuil, a Petite Cité de Caractère in the green north of the Périgord, above the Loue.

The château’s first stones date from around 1100; Richard the Lionheart besieged it twice. The town is named as Excidolium as early as 572.

There has been an inn here since 1751.
