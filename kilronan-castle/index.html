The Place · Ballyfarnon · Lough Meelagh

On Lough Meelagh

Kilronan stands in fifty acres of woodland on the shore of Lough Meelagh, near Ballyfarnon in north Roscommon.

The Hanly family began restoring the castle in 2004 and opened it as a hotel in 2008.

Boyle and Carrick-on-Shannon are twenty minutes away, Sligo thirty, and Ireland West Airport fifty.
