The Place · Menfi · Sicily

Menfi, vines to the sea

La Foresteria stands in the countryside outside Menfi, on Sicily’s south-west coast, among the Planeta family’s vineyards and olive groves.

The family has farmed in Sicily for seventeen generations; its wineries now reach from Menfi and Sambuca to Etna and Noto.

The dunes and pines of Lido Fiori are close by, and Palermo is about an hour and a half by car.
