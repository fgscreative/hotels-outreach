The Place · Bagno Vignoni · Val d’Orcia

At the springs

Le Terme faces the Piazza delle Sorgenti in Bagno Vignoni, where a thermal pool takes the place of a town square.

The palazzo was designed by Rossellino in the 15th century as a summer residence for Pope Pius II Piccolomini.

The Val d’Orcia, a UNESCO site, rolls away towards Pienza, Montalcino and San Quirico d’Orcia.
