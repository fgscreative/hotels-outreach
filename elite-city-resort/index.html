The Place · Kalamata · Messinian Gulf

On the Messinian Gulf

Elite City Resort lies on the seafront of Kalamata, on the long bay of the Messinian Gulf, with the Taygetos rising behind the town.

The city centre is 4 km away, the port 3 km, the airport 12 km.

Voidokilia, Methoni and the Mani are day trips along the coast.
