The Place · Eixample · Barcelona

In the Eixample

The apartments sit in the Eixample, the nineteenth-century grid of chamfered blocks laid out by Ildefons Cerdà, around Plaça de la Universitat.

La Rambla and La Boqueria are a short walk south; Passeig de Gràcia and Gaudí’s Casa Batlló a short walk north.

Each space keeps the building’s original features, reworked by the two designers who founded DestinationBCN.
