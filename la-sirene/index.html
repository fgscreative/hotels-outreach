The Place · Argelès-sur-Mer · Pays Catalan

In Argelès

La Sirène lies on the coastal plain of Argelès-sur-Mer, in the Pays Catalan, under the foothills of the Albères where the Pyrenees meet the sea.

The Carletti family bought 3.5 ha with two stars in 1967. Today the village covers 23 ha, nine hundred metres from a seven-kilometre beach.

Collioure and the Côte Vermeille are just to the south.
