The Place · Ebury Street · Belgravia, London

In Belgravia

Lime Tree stands on Ebury Street, a terrace of Georgian houses in Belgravia; a few doors along, at No. 180, the eight-year-old Mozart wrote his first symphony in 1764.

Sloane Square and the King’s Road are about ten minutes on foot; Buckingham Palace around fifteen, and the Chelsea Flower Show is held just down the road each May.

Victoria station is about five minutes away, with the Gatwick Express to the airport.
