The Place · Cowley · The Cotswolds

In the Churn valley

Cowley Manor stands in a fold of the Cotswold hills at Cowley, Gloucestershire, close to the springs where the River Churn rises.

Its fifty-five acres hold lawns, woodland and lakes; footpaths run out along the escarpment.

Cheltenham is about fifteen minutes by road; London around two hours.
