The Place · Bruges · West Flanders

Off the Markt

Hotel Navarra stands on Sint-Jakobsstraat, a hundred metres from the Markt and the Belfry, in the medieval centre of Bruges.

Don Juan de Peralta, consul of Navarra, lived here from around 1600; the house was redone in Louis XV style around 1720. Emperor Joseph II stayed in 1781, and Longfellow later.

The royal staircase and the Bonaparte rooms were made for Napoleon, who never came.
