The Place · Avenue de Friedland · Paris 8e

At the foot of the Arc

The hotel stands at 40 Avenue de Friedland, one of the twelve avenues that radiate from the Arc de Triomphe.

The avenue takes its name from Napoleon’s 1807 victory at Friedland; the building went up in 1928, in the Art Deco years.

The Champs-Élysées, Parc Monceau and the Faubourg Saint-Honoré are all an easy walk away.
