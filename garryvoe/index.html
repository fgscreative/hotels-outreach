The Place · Garryvoe · Ballycotton Bay

On Ballycotton Bay

The Garryvoe stands on the beach of Ballycotton Bay in East Cork, facing the lighthouse on its island across the water.

There has been a hotel on this strand since the early 1900s; the black lighthouse has stood since 1851.

Ballycotton’s cliff walk and harbour are just along the coast.
