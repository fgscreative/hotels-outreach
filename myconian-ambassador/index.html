The Place · Platis Gialos · Mykonos

Above the bay of Platis Gialos

The Ambassador climbs the granite hillside of Mykonos' south coast, facing the sheltered bay of Platis Gialos.

The meltemi, the island's summer north wind, arrives here softened — the hill takes its edge before it reaches your terrace.

Mykonos Town is ten minutes by car; the beaches of Psarou and Paraga, a walk.
