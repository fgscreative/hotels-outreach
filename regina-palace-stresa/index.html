The Place · Stresa · Lake Maggiore

On Lake Maggiore

The Regina Palace stands on Corso Umberto I, on the Stresa waterfront, facing the Borromean Islands of Lake Maggiore.

Architect Giuseppe Pagani built it in 1908 in the Art Nouveau style; the Stresa Conference met here in 1935, and Miss Italia was crowned here after the war.

Stresa station is 300 m away, Malpensa 45 km.
