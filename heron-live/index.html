On Lake Rożnów

Lake Rożnów is a reservoir on the Dunajec river, dammed in 1935–41, among the wooded foothills of Lesser Poland.

Heron Live stands on living rock at its edge, with forest on three sides and its own marina on the fourth.

Nowy Sącz is 15 km away and Kraków 102 km; Tropsztyn Castle and the Pieniny are nearby.
