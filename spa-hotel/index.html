The Place · Mount Ephraim · Royal Tunbridge Wells

On Mount Ephraim

The Spa Hotel stands on Mount Ephraim, beside the open heath of Tunbridge Wells Common, on the sandstone of the High Weald.

Fourteen acres of grounds hold three spring-fed lakes and mature trees.

The town grew around the Chalybeate Spring, found in 1606 — the Pantiles are a short walk downhill.
