The Place · Christchurch · Dorset

On Christchurch Harbour

Captain’s Club stands on the quay at Wick Ferry, where the River Stour runs into Christchurch Harbour.

Across the water lies Hengistbury Head; the Priory and the old town are a short walk away.

Bournemouth is about fifteen minutes by car, and the New Forest begins just inland.
