The Place · Porta Romana · Milano

A park inside Porta Romana

Grand Visconti Palace stands on Viale Isonzo, between Porta Romana and the Fondazione Prada, south-east of the Duomo.

Behind the façade lies a 2,500 m² park and winter garden — rare green space in the dense grid of central Milan.

The M3 metro at Lodi is close by, and the Duomo a few stops north.
