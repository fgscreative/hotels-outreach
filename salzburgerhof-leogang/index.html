The Place · Leogang · Salzburger Land

In Leogang

The Salzburger Hof stands on the Sonnberg above Leogang, facing the limestone wall of the Leoganger Steinberge.

The Asitzbahn leaves 99 metres away — into the Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn in winter, and into the Epic Bikepark in summer.

Anton and Anneliese Hörl opened it in 1988. The Panorama-Spa followed in 2021.
