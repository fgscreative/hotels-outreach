The Place · Merchants Quay · Waterford

On the Suir

Treacys stands at 1 Merchants Quay on the wide River Suir, in the centre of Waterford and across the river from the station.

Waterford is Ireland’s oldest city, founded by the Vikings; Reginald’s Tower and the Viking Triangle are along the quay.

The Waterford Greenway and the Copper Coast are within half an hour.
