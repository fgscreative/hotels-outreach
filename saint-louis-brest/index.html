The Place · Centre-ville · Brest

A harbour city at the end of France

Brest sits at the western tip of Brittany, on one of the largest natural harbours in Europe — the Rade de Brest.

The hotel is in the rebuilt heart of the city, between Place de la Liberté and the long slope of rue de Siam down to the water.

From the tram outside, the beaches, the Océanopolis aquarium and the boats to the islands are all within reach.
