The Place · Elia Beach · Mykonos

At Elia Beach

The Villa Collection climbs the hillside above Elia, one of the longest sandy beaches on the south coast of Mykonos, facing open Aegean water.

Mykonos Town and its windmills are about twenty minutes by car; boats to Delos, birthplace of Apollo in Greek myth, leave from the old port.

The airport is about fifteen minutes away; Platis Gialos, home of the sister Myconian Ambassador, around fifteen.
