The Place · Old Town · Prague 1

In the Old Town

The Maximilian stands on Haštalská, in the quiet northern corner of the Old Town, beside the Gothic church of St Haštal.

The Convent of St Agnes, founded in 1231, is round the corner; Pařížská street and the river are a few minutes’ walk.

Old Town Square, the Astronomical Clock and Charles Bridge are all within reach on foot.
