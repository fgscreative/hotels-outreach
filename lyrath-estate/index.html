The Place · Kilkenny · Ireland’s Ancient East

In 170 acres

Lyrath stands on the Dublin Road at the edge of Kilkenny, in 170 acres of lakes, historic gardens and woodland.

The land belonged to the Shortalls before 1653 and to the Tuppers until 1997; the botanist Lady Charlotte Wheeler-Cuffe made its Victorian garden.

Xavier McAuliffe bought the estate in 2003 and opened it in 2006, the old house joined to a contemporary wing.
