The Place · Baden-Baden · Black Forest

In Baden-Baden

Der Kleine Prinz stands on Lichtentaler Straße, between the Lichtentaler Allee and the old town of Baden-Baden, at the foot of the Black Forest.

The Friedrichsbad and the Caracalla baths, the casino and the Festspielhaus — one of Europe’s largest festival halls — are all within reach on foot.

The Merkur funicular climbs to the forest above the town.
