The Place · Agios Ioannis · Mykonos

On Agios Ioannis

Mykonos Grand stands on the bay of Agios Ioannis on the southwest coast of Mykonos — the beach of Shirley Valentine — looking across to sacred Delos.

Mykonos Town is 4.2 km away, with a free shuttle every evening; the airport 4.5 km.

Most of the team have been here six to ten years.
