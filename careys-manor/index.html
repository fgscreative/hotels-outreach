The Place · Brockenhurst · New Forest

In the New Forest

The New Forest has been a royal hunting ground since 1079. Charles II gave the old manor here to John Carey; the present house dates from 1888.

Before D-Day it housed the Welsh Guards and a Canadian headquarters. Today ponies graze the heath outside.

Brockenhurst station is a short walk; London Waterloo about ninety minutes by train.
