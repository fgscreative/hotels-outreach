The Place · Kensington · London

In South Kensington

Hotel Xenia stands on Cromwell Road in Kensington, in white stucco townhouses between Earl’s Court and South Kensington.

The Victoria and Albert Museum, the Natural History Museum and the Science Museum are a short walk east.

Hyde Park and Harrods are close by; Gloucester Road Tube station is about five minutes on foot.
