The Place · Llandderfel · Dee Valley, Wales

In the Dee Valley

Palé Hall stands above the River Dee near Llandderfel, a few miles from Bala, where the Dee Valley meets the hills of Snowdonia.

The house was built in 1871 for Henry Robertson, the Victorian railway engineer, and still keeps much of its original detail.

Bala Lake, the largest natural lake in Wales, is a short drive away; the peaks of Eryri rise to the west.
