The Place · New Bight · Cat Island, Bahamas

On Cat Island

Fernandez Bay Village sits on a mile-long white-sand beach just north of New Bight, on Cat Island in the central Bahamas.

Every room looks onto the bay; mangrove creeks, small cays and the Boiling Hole blue hole are close.

Makers Air flies in from Fort Lauderdale and Western Air from Nassau in thirty minutes, to New Bight’s paved runway five minutes away.
