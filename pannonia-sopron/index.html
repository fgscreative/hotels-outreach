The Place · Várkerület · Sopron

On the Várkerület

The Pannonia stands on the Várkerület, the ring road around Sopron’s walled baroque old town, at the foot of the Alps.

Móricz Hintráger designed the building in 1893. The Firewatch Tower and the main square are a few minutes’ walk away.

Lake Fertő and the vineyards lie to the east; Vienna is about 70 km.
