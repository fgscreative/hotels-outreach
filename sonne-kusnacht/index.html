The Place · Küsnacht · Lake Zurich

On Lake Zurich

The Sonne stands on the shore at Küsnacht, on the sunny “gold coast” of Lake Zurich, with its own boat jetty.

The Gasthaus zur Sonne was first recorded in 1641; its tower dates from the 13th century, and the Guggenbühl family kept the house for more than three hundred years.

Zurich main station is fifteen minutes by train; the airport is 18 km away.
