The Place · Saltsjö-Boo · Stockholm archipelago

In the inner archipelago

Skepparholmen lies in Saltsjö-Boo, Nacka, between the sea at Höggarnsfjärden and the lake Kvarnsjön, twenty minutes from central Stockholm.

In 1869 the timber merchant Francke built four villas here; Villa Torpa still stands. The spa opened in 2000 and its Scandinavian pools in 2015.

In summer the SL boat stops at Hasseludden jetty, a ten-minute walk away.
