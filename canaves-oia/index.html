The Place · Oia · Santorini

On the caldera rim

Oia sits at the northern tip of Santorini, on the rim of a caldera left by one of the largest volcanic eruptions in human history, around 1600 BC.

Below the village, about 300 steps lead down to the fishing harbour of Ammoudi.

Nea Kameni, the young volcano in the middle of the caldera, is a boat ride away, with warm springs off Palea Kameni.
