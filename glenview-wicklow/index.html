The Place · Glen of the Downs · Wicklow

In the Glen of the Downs

The hotel stands above the Glen of the Downs, a steep glacial valley whose oak woods are a nature reserve.

It opened in 1914 as a five-bedroom hunting lodge on the Powerscourt Estate. The Great Sugar Loaf rises just to the west.

Dublin is about 30 km away; Bray and the sea are closer still.
