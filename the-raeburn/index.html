The Place · Stockbridge · Edinburgh

In Stockbridge

The Raeburn stands on Raeburn Place in Stockbridge, the village quarter of Edinburgh’s New Town, named for the painter Sir Henry Raeburn.

The Water of Leith runs a few streets away; the Royal Botanic Garden and Inverleith Park are a short walk north.

Princes Street and the Old Town are about twenty minutes on foot; the Sunday market is round the corner.
