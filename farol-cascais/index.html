The Place · Cascais · Estoril Coast

On the Cascais rocks

Farol means lighthouse: the hotel stands beside the Santa Marta lighthouse, on the rocks where Cascais bay meets the Atlantic.

The marina and the old town are a short walk away; Guincho beach and the Sintra hills a short drive.

Lisbon is about thirty minutes by car, or forty by the coastal train from Cascais station.
