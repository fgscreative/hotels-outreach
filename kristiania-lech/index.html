The Place · Lech am Arlberg · Austria

Above Lech

Kristiania stands on the Omesberg, just above the village of Lech, at about 1,480 m in the Arlberg — often called the cradle of alpine skiing.

Ski Arlberg links Lech, Zürs, St. Anton and Warth-Schröcken: around 300 km of pistes, with the Weißer Ring circuit starting in the village.

Langen am Arlberg station is about fifteen minutes away; Innsbruck around 1 h 30, and Zurich about 2 h 30 by road.
