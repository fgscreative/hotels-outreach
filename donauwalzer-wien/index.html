The Place · Hernalser Gürtel · Wien

On the Gürtel, under Otto Wagner's arches

The hotel stands on the Hernalser Gürtel, the ring road that once marked the outer edge of imperial Vienna.

Alongside runs the U6, on the arched viaduct Otto Wagner designed for the Stadtbahn in the 1890s.

The city centre is 1,600 metres away — a short ride on our free bicycles.
