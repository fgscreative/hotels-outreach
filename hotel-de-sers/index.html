The Place · Triangle d’Or · Paris 8e

In the Triangle d’Or

Hôtel de Sers stands at 41 Avenue Pierre 1er de Serbie, in the Golden Triangle of the 8th arrondissement.

The Champs-Élysées, Avenue George V and Avenue Montaigne frame the quarter; the Seine is at the bottom of the street.

The Arc de Triomphe and the Palais de Tokyo are each a short walk away.
