The Place · Kamari Beach · Santorini

On Kamari Beach

Kamari lies on Santorini’s east coast, a long beach of dark volcanic sand beneath the limestone bulk of Mesa Vouno.

The ruins of Ancient Thera crown the summit; the airport is about ten minutes by car, Fira about fifteen.

Hotel Matina sits in its own garden, a short walk from the seafront promenade and its tavernas.
