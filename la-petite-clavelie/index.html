The Place · Ribérac · Périgord Vert

In the Périgord Vert

La Petite Clavelie is a working farm outside Ribérac, in the green north of the Dordogne, with vegetables, jams and a donkey.

Brantôme, Aubeterre, Saint-Jean-de-Côle and Bourdeilles are all close; Lascaux is a day out.

Ribérac holds its market every Friday.
