The Place · Wattwiller · Alsace

In the Ballons des Vosges

The domaine sits at 550 m in the Hirtzenstein forest, inside the Ballons des Vosges regional nature park, above the village of Wattwiller.

It stands where the Route des Vins meets the Route des Crêtes: vineyards below, the high Vosges ridges and the Grand Ballon above.

Cernay station is ten minutes away; Basel-Mulhouse airport about forty.
