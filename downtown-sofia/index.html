The Place · Sofia · City Centre

In central Sofia

Hotel Downtown stands on Vasil Levski Boulevard in the centre of Sofia, a walk from the National Palace of Culture.

The city lies at about 550 metres in a high basin; Vitosha rises straight to the south, its summit at 2,290 metres.

The airport is 7.5 km away, about twenty minutes by car.
