The Place · Berkeley Square · Mayfair

On Berkeley Square

The Club stands discreetly at the south-west corner of Berkeley Square, in Mayfair, at the heart of London’s West End.

Lansdowne House was designed by Robert Adam in the 1760s; in 1935 it became a members’ club open to men and women on equal terms.

Green Park, Bond Street and Piccadilly are all a few minutes’ walk away.
