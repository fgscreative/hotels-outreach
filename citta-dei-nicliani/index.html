The Place · Kita · Mani

In the Mani

Citta dei Nicliani stands in Kita, a village of stone towers in the Mani, the southern finger of the Peloponnese, above the Messinian Gulf.

The final talks that ended the last great Mani vendetta, in 1871, took place in this tower; later it housed the Royal Gendarmerie.

It lay empty until 2011, when the family restored it. A Byzantine church of 1170 stands three hundred metres away.
