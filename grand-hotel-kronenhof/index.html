The Place · Pontresina · Engadin

In Pontresina

The Kronenhof stands in Pontresina, at 1,805 m in the Upper Engadin, where the Val Roseg opens toward the village and the Bernina massif rises beyond.

Larch and Swiss stone pine cover the slopes; Piz Bernina, at 4,049 m the highest peak of the Eastern Alps, and the Morteratsch glacier are a short trip up the valley.

St. Moritz is about ten minutes by car, and the Bernina line of the Rhaetian Railway — a UNESCO World Heritage route — stops in the village.
