The Place · Basel · Rhine

On the Rhine

The inn “Drei Könige” was first mentioned in 1681. In 1754 three wooden kings went up on the façade; Napoleon stopped here in 1797.

The hotel stands on the Rhine bank in the Old Town, beside the Mittlere Brücke, with the river terrace below the rooms.

Empress Michiko, James Joyce and Picasso have all stayed.
