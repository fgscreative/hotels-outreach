The Place · Windermere · Lake District

Above Windermere

Cedar Manor stands on Ambleside Road at the edge of Windermere town, inside the Lake District National Park.

Orrest Head, Alfred Wainwright’s first fell, is a short walk away; the lake shore at Bowness is about a mile downhill.

Windermere station is about ten minutes on foot, with direct trains to Oxenholme and Manchester Airport.
