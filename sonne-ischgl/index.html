The Place · Ischgl · Paznaun, Tyrol

In the Paznaun

Hotel Sonne stands on the Dorfstraße in the car-free centre of Ischgl, at 1,377 metres in the Paznaun valley, beside the Trisanna.

The village tunnel leads straight to the gondolas of the Silvretta Arena: 238 kilometres of slopes and 45 lifts, across the border to Samnaun.

The snow usually holds from late November to early May.
