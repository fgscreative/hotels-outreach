The Place · Kurfürstendamm · Charlottenburg

On the Kurfürstendamm

Louisa’s Place stands at Kurfürstendamm 160, in a listed city villa of 1904 at the western end of Berlin’s great boulevard, in Charlottenburg.

The Memorial Church is 2.5 km away; the Messe and City Cube 3.2 km.

Every room is dedicated to Queen Louise of Prussia.
