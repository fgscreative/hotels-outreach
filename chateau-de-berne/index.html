The Place · Lorgues · Provence

In the Var hills

The estate lies between Lorgues and Flayosc in the Var, 515 ha of forest, vines and olives on a plateau at around 300 m.

A branch of the Roman Via Aurelia crossed it; in the 12th century Raymond V gave the land to Bernard de Clairvaux.

Today 150 ha of vines are farmed organically, and Saint-Tropez is about an hour away.
