The Place · Castelraimondo · Marche hills

In the Marche hills

Borgo Lanciano sits in the green hills above Castelraimondo, where the Marche climbs toward the Apennines and the Monti Sibillini.

The manor was once the home of Princess Giustiniani; today Vittorina and Giovanni Zuffellato run it, with their own organic wines.

The larder is local: ciauscolo salami, saffron from Cascia and trout from Sefro.
