The Place · Tettenhall Wood · Wolverhampton

In Tettenhall Wood

The Mount stands in wooded grounds on the western edge of Wolverhampton, where the city gives way to Staffordshire farmland.

The Mander family bought it for £5,000 in 1890. Charles Tertius Mander became a baronet in 1911; the family’s other house, Wightwick Manor, now the National Trust’s, is less than a kilometre away.

Queen Mary stayed twice. The house became a hotel in 1952.
