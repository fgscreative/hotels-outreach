The Place · Vecrīga · Riga

In Old Riga

Neiburgs stands on Jauniela in Vecrīga, Riga’s medieval Old Town and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, beside the Dom Cathedral.

The House of the Blackheads, St Peter’s Church and the Three Brothers are each a few minutes’ walk.

The Daugava embankment is a short walk; the airport is about twenty minutes by car.
