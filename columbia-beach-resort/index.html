The Place · Pissouri Bay · Cyprus

On Pissouri Bay

Columbia Beach Resort is terraced above Pissouri Bay, a quiet curve of the south coast between Limassol and Paphos.

The chalk cliffs of Cape Aspro close the bay to the west; the village of Pissouri sits on the hill above.

The resort opened in 2005 and was renewed in 2017, in seven and a half hectares of gardens.
