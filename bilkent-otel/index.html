The Place · Bilkent · Ankara

In Bilkent

Bilkent Otel stands in green grounds on the Ankara plateau, beside Bilkent University in the Çankaya district.

It opened in 1991 as Ankara’s first congress hotel. It belongs to Bilkent Holding, whose income supports the university and its scholarships.

The city centre at Kızılay is about twenty minutes away.
