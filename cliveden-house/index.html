The Place · Taplow · Berkshire

Above the Thames

Cliveden stands on a bluff above the Thames at Taplow, its parterre and hanging woods running down to Cliveden Reach.

The 2nd Duke of Buckingham built it in 1666; every reigning monarch since George I has visited. The grounds belong to the National Trust.

London is about forty minutes away; by vintage launch Marlow is two hours upriver.
