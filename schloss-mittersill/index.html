The Place · Mittersill · Hohe Tauern

Above the Salzach

Schloss Mittersill stands on a terrace above the Salzach valley, between the Hohe Tauern National Park and the Kitzbühel Alps.

The Counts of Lechsgemünd built it around 1150. It burned in the peasant uprising of 1526; the year 1528 is carved above the archway.

From 1948 it was the Sport & Shooting Club: Clark Gable, Rita Hayworth and the Duke of Windsor stayed, and Queen Juliana spent her honeymoon here.
