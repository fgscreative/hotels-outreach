The Place · Killarney · Co. Kerry

Beside the park

The Killarney Park stands at the edge of the town, two minutes’ walk from the gate of Killarney National Park.

The park’s 26,000 acres of oak woods and lakes are a UNESCO biosphere, home to Ireland’s only wild native red deer, peregrines and white-tailed eagles.

Padraig and Janet Treacy bought the site in 1990; their son Marcus runs it now, and the family’s first guesthouse, The Ross, is in the town.
