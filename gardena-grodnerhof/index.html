In Val Gardena

The Gardena stands in Ortisei, the main village of Val Gardena, at 1,236 m in the Dolomites of South Tyrol.

Anna and Anton Demetz opened their house to guests in 1923; Hugo and Cinzia Bernardi have run it since 2000 and rebuilt it in 2001.

The Seceda and the Alpe di Siusi rise above the valley, and the Sella Ronda starts close by.
