The Place · Minster Quarter · York

Between Minster and walls

Grays Court stands on Chapter House Street in York’s Minster Quarter, on the site of the Roman fortress.

Its gardens run to the city walls, which guests can step onto directly; York Minster is just beyond the gate.

The Shambles is a few minutes on foot; York station is about fifteen.
