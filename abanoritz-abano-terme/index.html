The Place · Abano Terme · Colli Euganei

At the foot of Monte Ortone

AbanoRitz stands on Via Monteortone, on the edge of Abano Terme, where the Euganean Hills — a ring of extinct volcanoes — rise from the Venetian plain.

Rain that falls on the Pre-Alps sinks deep underground and travels for years before surfacing here, hot and rich in salts.

The same heat works for the hotel: AbanoRitz draws on geothermal energy from the springs beneath it.
