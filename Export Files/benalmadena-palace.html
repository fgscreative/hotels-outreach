The Place

Benalmádena,
where the sun
clocks out last.

Positioned on Benalmádena Costa — one of the most sun-saturated stretches of southern Spain — the Palace sits where the Sierra de Mijas descends to the sea. 325 days of sunlight per year. This is not a claim. It is geography.

The Mediterranean at this latitude runs between 18°C and 26°C. The sea-facing rooms catch the afternoon light as it bends off the water, long and amber, the kind that makes every hour feel like the last golden one.

The beach begins 250 metres from the lobby. The Parque de la Paloma — with its subtropical gardens — begins just beyond. This is the texture of a well-chosen place.
