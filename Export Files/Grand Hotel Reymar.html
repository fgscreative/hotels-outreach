Tossa de Mar · Costa Brava

A coast that
resists erasure

The Costa Brava folds hard into the Mediterranean at Tossa. Limestone cliffs carved over millennia by the Tramuntana. A medieval walled town intact since the 12th century. The sea here has a colour that resists translation.

Below the surface, Posidonia oceanica meadows filter the water into aquamarine. Above, Gran Hotel Reymar occupies the headland — close enough to feel spray on still mornings, elevated enough to hold the full horizon at sunset.

Forty minutes from Barcelona El Prat. A different measure of time entirely.
