Fouras · Charente-Maritime

Presque une île. Entièrement un territoire.

The Fouras peninsula extends into the Pertuis charentais like a slow inhale. La Rochelle is thirty-five kilometres north. Île d'Aix is a short crossing. Fort Boyard, visible from the hotel garden, rises and falls with the Charente's tides.

Four kilometres of fine Atlantic sand across four named beaches. A chêne vert oak forest, three working harbours, a morning market that has run without interruption since before the hotel was built in 1890.

The peninsula's tempo is measured by the tide, not the clock. That is why every stay here begins and ends at the spa — where the rhythm is already set.
