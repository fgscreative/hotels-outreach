Benidorm sits at 38° north, inside a protected bay flanked on both sides by headlands. The Sierra Helada rises to 439 metres on the eastern edge of the beach. The result is a microclimate — 320 days of sun per year, sea temperatures that stay above 20°C from May to November, and a horizon that belongs exclusively to this bay.

The Playa de Levante and Playa de Poniente stretch across 3 km of sand, both Blue Flag certified every year since 1988. Between them, the old town sits on a limestone promontory 80 metres above the water. From the Hotel Presidente, both beaches are within 300 metres on foot.

Four Hoteles Benidorm properties anchor the northern arc of the bay. Benicaldea thermal spa, at Calle Gerona 39-41, opens daily until 10 pm. Seven bars span the ground-level entertainment district — all reachable by internal corridor. The sea and the schedule are yours to arrange.
