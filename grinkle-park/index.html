The Place · Easington · North York Moors

Between moor and sea

Grinkle Park lies near Easington, between the North York Moors and the coast, in parkland of lakes and woods.

Alfred Waterhouse designed it in 1881 for Sir Charles Mark Palmer, once the world’s largest shipbuilder. It became a hotel in 1947.

Staithes, Whitby with its 199 steps, Saltburn’s pier and Roseberry Topping are all close by.
