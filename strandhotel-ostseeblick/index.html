The Place · Heringsdorf · Usedom

On Usedom

Heringsdorf is one of Usedom’s three imperial seaside resorts, linked to Ahlbeck and Bansin by a beach promenade about twelve kilometres long.

The hotel stands in its front row, with white sand and the Baltic straight ahead.

Heringsdorf’s pier, 508 m, is the longest in Germany; the Polish border is a short ride east.
