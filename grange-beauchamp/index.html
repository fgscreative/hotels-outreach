The Place · Bloomsbury · London

In Bloomsbury

Bedford Place is a Georgian street that runs between Bloomsbury Square and Russell Square, two of London’s garden squares.

The British Museum is around the corner; Covent Garden and the West End are a short walk south.

Holborn and Russell Square stations are both within walking distance.
