The Place · Lechlade · Cotswolds

In Coln Park

The Lakes sit near Lechlade, where the Thames becomes navigable, in the gravel country of the Cotswold Water Park.

The estate was a quarry. John Hitchcox and YOO turned it into 850 acres of lakes, reed beds and woodland, with homes, cabins and a hotel at the water’s edge.

London is about ninety minutes away.
