The Place · Austwick · Yorkshire Dales

Below Ingleborough

The Traddock stands at the edge of Austwick, a stone village in the Yorkshire Dales National Park, a few miles from Settle.

Ingleborough, 723 m, rises to the north; the Norber erratics — glacial boulders left on limestone stems — lie an hour’s walk away.

Drystone walls, hay meadows and Austwick Beck shape the walks that begin at our gate.
