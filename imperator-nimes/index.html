The Place · Nîmes · Jardins de la Fontaine

In Roman Nîmes

L’Imperator stands on the Quai de la Fontaine, beside the 18th-century Jardins de la Fontaine, in the heart of Nîmes.

The Maison Carrée and the Roman arena are a short walk away; the bullfighters and their admirers came here for the Ferias.

Behind the walls, a hundred-year-old ginkgo shades the garden.
