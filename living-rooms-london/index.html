The Place · Marylebone · Mayfair · Notting Hill

Four neighbourhoods, one front door

Living Rooms began in 1992 with one Mayfair townhouse at No. 5 Maddox Street, a few doors from Regent Street.

Today the collection spans Marylebone, Mayfair, Notting Hill and Little Venice — Regency terraces, garden squares and a canal.

Each address keeps the character of its street, with hotel service behind every door.
