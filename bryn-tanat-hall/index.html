The Place · Llansantffraid · Powys

In the Welsh Marches

Bryn Tanat Hall stands in fifteen acres of river-fronted gardens and parkland near Llansantffraid-ym-Mechain, in the north of Powys.

Shrewsbury and Chester are each less than an hour away; the North Wales coast about two.

Eryri (Snowdonia) National Park is around seventy minutes up the A5.
