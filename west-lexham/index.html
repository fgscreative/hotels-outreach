The Place · Lexham · Nar Valley, Norfolk

In the valley

West Lexham lies in a quiet valley north of Swaffham, where a restored chalk stream runs through a water meadow protected as a Site of Special Scientific Interest.

The estate draws its own spring water and runs on more than ninety per cent renewable energy.

The name, the estate says, means “place of the healer” in Old English.
