The Place · Nuévalos · Aragón

On the río Piedra

In 1186 Alfonso II and Queen Sancha gave this gorge to the Cistercians; twelve monks left Poblet in 1194 and stayed for more than six hundred years.

In 1843 Pablo Muntadas bought the monastery at auction; his family turned the river gorge into a garden of waterfalls, lakes and grottoes.

It became a Paraje Pintoresco in 1945 and a Jardín Histórico in 2010.
