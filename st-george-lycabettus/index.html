The Place · Kolonaki · Athens

On Lycabettus

The hotel stands on the lower slopes of Lycabettus, the pine-covered limestone hill that rises 277 m above the centre of Athens.

It shares its name with the Chapel of St. George on the summit. Kolonaki, the city’s gallery and café quarter, starts at the front door.

The Acropolis is a 20–30 minute walk; Syntagma about fifteen.
