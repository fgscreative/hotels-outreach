The Place · Ambleside · Lake District

In Ambleside

Rothay Manor stands on Rothay Road at the southern edge of Ambleside, inside the Lake District National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Waterhead, at the head of Windermere, is about ten minutes on foot; the rivers Rothay and Brathay meet in the meadows nearby.

Grasmere is about fifteen minutes by road, and Windermere station around twenty.
