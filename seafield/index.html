The Place · Ballymoney · North Wexford coast

Above Ballymoney

Seafield sits in 160 acres of wooded parkland above Ballymoney beach, on the north Wexford coast near Gorey.

Green farmland runs down to a sandy cove on the Irish Sea, with Courtown a few minutes along the shore.

Dublin is about an hour north.
