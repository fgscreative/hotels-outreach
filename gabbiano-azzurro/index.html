On Terza Spiaggia

Terza Spiaggia is the largest of Golfo Aranci’s five beaches — a shallow, sheltered lagoon on Sardinia’s north-east coast.

The granite headland of Capo Figari rises behind it; across the water lies the island of Tavolara.

The Costa Smeralda and Olbia are each about twenty minutes away.
