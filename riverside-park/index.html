The Place · Enniscorthy · River Slaney

Below the castle

The Riverside Park stands on the Promenade at the south edge of Enniscorthy, on the bank of the River Slaney.

Enniscorthy Castle looks down on the river, and Vinegar Hill rises above the town.

Wexford has over 275 km of coastline; Curracloe and Rosslare Europort are within the hour.
