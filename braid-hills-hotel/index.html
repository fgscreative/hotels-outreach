The Place · Braid Hills · Edinburgh

On the Braid Hills

Braid Hills Hotel stands on Braid Road in Morningside, on the slope of the Braid Hills, with views north over Edinburgh to the castle.

Braidburn Valley Park is next door, the wooded glen of the Hermitage of Braid a short walk away, and the Pentland Hills on the skyline.

The city centre is about eight minutes by car or twenty by the bus that stops outside; parking is free.
