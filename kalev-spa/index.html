The Place · Old Town · Tallinn

At the Old Town’s edge

Kalev Spa stands on Aia street in central Tallinn, at the eastern edge of the medieval Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Viru Gate is a few minutes on foot and Town Hall Square a few more; the harbour and its ferries to Helsinki about ten.

Tallinn airport is around fifteen minutes by taxi.
