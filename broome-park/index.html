The Place · Barham · Kent

A house from the 1630s

Broome Park was completed around 1638, in the reign of Charles I, and is one of the finest Carolean houses in Kent.

Lord Kitchener bought the estate in 1911. Its mansion, woods and parkland lie in the Elham Valley on the North Downs.

Canterbury and its cathedral are about fifteen minutes north by car; the white cliffs of Dover about twenty.
