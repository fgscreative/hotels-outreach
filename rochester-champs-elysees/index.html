The Place · Paris 8e · Champs-Élysées

Off the Champs-Élysées

The Rochester stands on rue La Boétie in the 8th arrondissement, between the Faubourg Saint-Honoré and the Élysée Palace.

The Champs-Élysées are five minutes on foot; the Grand and Petit Palais ten.

Through a revolving door with copper uprights, gold damask and skylights, the hall leads to the patio.
