The Place · Sneem · Ring of Kerry

On Kenmare Bay

Parknasilla lies in 500 acres of islands, inlets and hidden beaches on the north shore of Kenmare Bay, just east of Sneem on the Ring of Kerry.

James Franklin Fuller designed the manor for the Great Southern & Western Railway; it took its first guests in 1895, and George Bernard Shaw stayed in 1925.

Killarney is 41 km away, over Moll’s Gap and Ladies View.
