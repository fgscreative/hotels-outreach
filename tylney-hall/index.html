The Place · Rotherwick · North Hampshire

In 66 acres

Tylney Hall stands on a low ridge near the village of Rotherwick, in north Hampshire parkland planted with more than a quarter of a million trees and shrubs.

Sir Lionel Phillips bought the estate in 1898. The Great Hall and Italian Lounge ceilings came from the Grimani Palace in Venice; the gardens follow Gertrude Jekyll.

It was a hospital in the First World War and a school until 1984. It opened as a hotel in 1985.
