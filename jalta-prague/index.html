The Place · Wenceslas Square · Prague

At the top of Wenceslas Square

Jalta stands on Wenceslas Square, a few steps below the National Museum, behind a façade of carved stone and balconies designed by Antonín Tenzer.

Completed in 1958, it is a protected landmark of post-war Czech architecture.

The Old Town, the Astronomical Clock and Charles Bridge are a fifteen-minute walk away.
