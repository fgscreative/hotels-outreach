The Place · Ballantrae · Ayrshire

Above the Firth of Clyde

Glenapp Castle stands on a wooded rise south of Ballantrae in south-west Scotland, looking out over the outer Firth of Clyde to Ailsa Craig.

The castle was built in 1870 to designs by David Bryce, set in 110 acres of gardens and woodland.

The ferry port at Cairnryan is about fifteen minutes away; Glasgow around two hours by road.
