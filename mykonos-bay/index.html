The Place · Megali Ammos · Mykonos

On Megali Ammos

Mykonos Bay Resort lies on Megali Ammos, the long sandy shore just south of Mykonos Town.

The town and its bus station are 300 m away, the port 1 km and the airport 2 km.

Built in 1985, opened in 1986 and renewed in 2018, it is filled with Tinos marble and the family’s art.
