The Place · Beemster · North Holland

In the Beemster

Fort Resort Beemster stands in the Beemster, a polder drained in 1612 and laid out in a grid of roads, dykes and ditches — a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The fort belongs to the Defence Line of Amsterdam, a ring of forts built around 1900 to flood the land against an army.

Amsterdam is about thirty minutes by car; Purmerend station around ten.
