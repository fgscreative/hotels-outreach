The Place · Montanejos · Alto Mijares

In the heart of Montanejos

El Palacio stands in the centre of Montanejos, a village of the Alto Mijares in inland Castellón, in a landmark building restored by the family.

The Balneario is a three-minute walk; upstream, the Fuente de los Baños fills river pools at 25 °C all year.

Around the village, limestone gorges and the Arenós reservoir draw walkers, climbers and swimmers.
