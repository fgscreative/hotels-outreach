The Place · Vernantois · Jura

At the foot of the Revermont

The domaine lies in the Sorne valley at Vernantois, at the foot of the Revermont, ten minutes from Lons-le-Saunier.

It was the Moulin des Cuchots, then a nine-hole course. In 1989 Christian de Grivel gathered land from about sixty families of three villages; the course opened in March 1991.

The Hérisson waterfalls, Baume-les-Messieurs and Château-Chalon are all within reach.
