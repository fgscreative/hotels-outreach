The Place · Corrubbio · Valpolicella, Verona

In the hills of Valpolicella

Villa Amistà stands at Corrubbio, among the Valpolicella hills on the edge of Verona, on the remains of a Roman fortified house.

Its central body dates from the sixteenth century and is attributed to Michele Sanmicheli; the villa we see today was rebuilt around 1700 by Ignazio Pellegrini.

Outside, the Amarone and Ripasso vineyards climb the hills; Verona and its Arena are about twenty minutes away.
