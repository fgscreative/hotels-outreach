The Place · Boninovo · Dubrovnik

Above Boninovo

Hotel Lero stands on the hillside of Boninovo, between Lapad and the walls of Dubrovnik, above a small bay of the Adriatic.

Pile Gate and the Old Town are 1.7 km away — a fifteen-minute walk along the sea, or four stops on the number 4 bus from outside.

The building went up in 1971 as a student dormitory and opened as a hotel a year later.
