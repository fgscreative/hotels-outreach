The Place · Anacapri · Capri

On the cliffs of Anacapri

Caesar Augustus stands on the cliffs of Anacapri, the island’s upper village, looking out over the Bay of Naples to Vesuvius.

Built as a private villa in the nineteenth century, it now holds 56 rooms and suites, a two-level infinity pool and gardens along the cliff.

The centre of Anacapri is less than two hundred metres away; Capri’s Piazzetta is ten minutes by taxi or bus.
