The Place · Los Cristianos · Tenerife

On Los Cristianos bay

Arona Gran stands on the seafront of Los Cristianos in south Tenerife, behind black lava breakwaters.

La Gomera lies across the water — the ferries leave from the harbour next door — and Teide, 3,715 m, rises inland.

Tenerife South airport is 17 km away, about twenty minutes.
