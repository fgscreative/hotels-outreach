The Place · San Felice Circeo · Lazio

Under Monte Circeo

The hotel stands on the seafront of San Felice Circeo, about 100 km south of Rome, inside the Circeo National Park.

Behind it rises Monte Circeo, the limestone promontory where legend places the island of the sorceress Circe.

The park, founded in 1934, also protects the coastal dunes, four lakes and the Sabaudia forest.
