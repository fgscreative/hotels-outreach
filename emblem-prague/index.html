The Place · Old Town · Prague

Steps from Old Town Square

The Emblem stands on Platnéřská, a quiet street in Prague’s Old Town between Old Town Square and the Vltava.

The building is Art Deco; inside, the rooms are calm and contemporary, hung with modern art and finished with thoughtful detail.

The Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge and the Jewish Quarter are all a short walk away.
