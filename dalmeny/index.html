The Place · Lytham St Annes · Lancashire

On the Fylde Coast

The Dalmeny stands on the Victorian South Promenade at St Annes, facing a wide beach of sand and dunes on the Irish Sea.

The Ribble estuary lies to the south; Lytham’s windmill and green are about three miles along the coast.

Blackpool is about five miles north.
