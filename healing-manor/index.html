The Place · Healing · North East Lincolnshire

Between the Wolds and the Humber

Healing Manor stands in 36 acres of parkland on the edge of a village recorded in the Domesday Book of 1086.

To the north runs the wide Humber estuary; to the south-west rise the chalk hills of the Lincolnshire Wolds.

Grimsby, once the busiest fishing port in the world, is ten minutes east.
