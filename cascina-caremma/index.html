The Place · Besate · Parco del Ticino

In the Ticino Park

Cascina Caremma lies in the fields south of Besate, inside the Parco del Ticino, about 30 km south-west of Milan.

Its 36 hectares are farmed organically in rotation, and the old barn now holds the spa.

The Ticino woods and the Navigli towpaths are close for walking and cycling; the abbey of Morimondo is a few kilometres away.
