On Santa Eulària’s bay

Aguas de Ibiza stands on the promenade of Santa Eulària des Riu, the calm town on Ibiza’s east coast, above its marina.

The beaches of the east coast and the hill villages of the interior are short drives away.

Ibiza town is about twenty minutes by road; the airport around twenty-five.
