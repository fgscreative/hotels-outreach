The Place · Capri · near the Piazzetta

Above the Piazzetta

The Tiberio Palace stands on Via Croce, a car-free lane five minutes’ walk from the Piazzetta and the funicular.

Below the town, limestone cliffs drop to the Faraglioni and the Blue Grotto; Anacapri and Monte Solaro rise to the west.

Ferries from Naples and Sorrento land at Marina Grande, where the hotel meets its guests.
