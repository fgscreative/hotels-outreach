The Place · Ballina · River Moy

Above the river

Mount Falcon stands in 100 acres on the west bank of the River Moy, on the Foxford Road just south of Ballina.

The house was built in 1876 as a wedding gift and turns 150 this year; its own stretch of the Moy is fished for salmon.

It is a base for the Wild Atlantic Way — Downpatrick Head, the Céide Fields, Achill and Keem Bay.
