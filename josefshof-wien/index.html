The Place · Josefstadt · Wien

In Josefstadt

Hotel Josefshof stands on Josefsgasse, a quiet side street in Josefstadt, Vienna’s 8th district, just behind the Rathaus.

The Ringstrasse, the Burgtheater and the Theater in der Josefstadt are a few minutes away on foot.

The U2 at Rathaus and the trams on the Ring connect the house to the rest of the city.
