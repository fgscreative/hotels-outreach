The Place · Barton · Preston · Lancashire

Garstang Road, north of Preston

Barton Manor began as a manor house built in 1900 by the Lancastrian Topping and Mellors families, on the road north from Preston.

Today it is a 103-room hotel with a spa and leisure club, still family-owned and still on Garstang Road.

The Forest of Bowland rises to the east; the Lake District is an hour north up the M6.
