The Place · Sorrento · Campania

A balcony over the Bay of Naples

Bellevue Syrene stands on the tuff cliffs of Sorrento — volcanic rock laid down by the Campanian eruption 39,000 years ago.

Every terrace looks west to Capri and north to Vesuvius, the bay turning from silver at dawn to cobalt by noon.

Below, a private lift drops through the rock to the solarium and the clear water at the foot of the cliff.
