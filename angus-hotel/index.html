The Place · Blairgowrie · Perthshire

In Blairgowrie

The Angus faces the Wellmeadow, the green at the heart of Blairgowrie; some rooms look over the River Ericht.

This is the edge of Highland Perthshire, Scotland’s Big Tree Country. Dunkeld, Scone Palace and Glamis are within half an hour; Glenshee, Braemar and Balmoral within the hour.

Dundee airport is thirty minutes away, Edinburgh an hour and a quarter.
