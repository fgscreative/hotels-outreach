The Place · Kypri Bay · Andros

On Andros

Hotel Perrakis sits above Kypri beach on the west coast of Andros, the northernmost of the Cyclades, between Kypri and Chryssi Ammos.

Gavrio, where the ferries from Rafina arrive, is 2.8 km away.

Every one of the 44 rooms has a balcony facing the sea.
