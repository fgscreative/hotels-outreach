The Place · Kastelruth · Dolomites

In Kastelruth

Schgaguler stands on the square of Kastelruth, a village at about 1,060 m on a sunny plateau of the western Dolomites.

The Seiser Alm — around 56 km² of high pasture — spreads above it beneath the Schlern, part of the Dolomites World Heritage site since 2009.

Val Gardena is about twenty minutes by car; Bolzano around thirty-five.
