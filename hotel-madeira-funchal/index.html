The Place · Funchal · Madeira

In old Funchal

Hotel Madeira stands on Rua Ivens in the centre of Funchal, a few streets up from the marina and the seafront.

Behind the city the hills climb to Pico do Arieiro; the laurel forest of the levadas is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The Lavradores market, the cathedral and the cable car up to Monte are all within walking distance.
