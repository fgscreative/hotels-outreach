Facing Mont Ventoux

Crillon-le-Brave is a small perched village in the Vaucluse, looking straight at the south face of Mont Ventoux across vines and olive groves.

The hotel is a dozen 17th- and 18th-century houses around the church, linked by lanes and terraces; the spa fills the old stables and the gym the village chapel.

Carpentras strawberries, Ventoux goat’s cheese and Alpilles lamb come to the kitchen.
