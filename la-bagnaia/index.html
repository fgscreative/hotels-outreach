The Place · Murlo · Siena hills

In the Siena hills

La Bagnaia lies in the hills of Murlo, about 15 km south of Siena on the road to Grosseto.

The resort is a restored medieval hamlet within 130 hectares of golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr.

The Crete Senesi, Montalcino and the thermal square of Bagno Vignoni are all within the hour.
