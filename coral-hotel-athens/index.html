The Place · Palaio Faliro · Athenian Riviera

On Faliro Bay

The Coral stands on Poseidonos Avenue in Palaio Faliro, facing Faliro bay on the Saronic Gulf, at the start of the Athenian Riviera.

Flisvos Marina is a few steps away, the park a short walk; the Acropolis lies inland, Piraeus along the coast.

One family has welcomed guests here since 1963.
