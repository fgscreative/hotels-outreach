The Place · Oltrarno · Florence

On the Oltrarno

Villa Cora stands on Viale Machiavelli, on the green slope south of the Arno near Porta Romana, looking toward the Boboli Gardens.

Built for Baron Gustave Oppenheim, it later belonged to Empress Eugénie; Egidio Cora bought it in 1894. Debussy stayed here.

The Ponte Vecchio is a twenty-minute walk; a shuttle runs to the centre.
