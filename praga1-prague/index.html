The Place · Karlovo náměstí · Prague 1

On Charles Square

Praga 1 stands on Žitná at the top of Karlovo náměstí, the square Charles IV laid out as the New Town’s cattle market in 1348.

Today it is a park of plane trees and fountains, and one of the largest squares in Europe.

Wenceslas Square and the National Theatre are each a short walk away.
