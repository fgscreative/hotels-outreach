The Place · Skipton · Yorkshire Dales edge

On the edge of the Dales

Broughton lies two miles west of Skipton, where the Yorkshire Dales begin — heather moor, old and new woodland, meadows and rivers.

The Tempest family has lived here since 1097; Roger Tempest is the 32nd generation. Over a thousand hectares are turning from sheep pasture back to mixed habitat.

More than 200,000 trees went in during the winter of 2020–21.
