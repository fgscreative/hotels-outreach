The Place · Les Giettes · Valais

Facing the Dents du Midi

Whitepod sits above Monthey in the canton of Valais, at Les Cerniers on the slopes of Les Giettes, facing the Dents du Midi.

Reception and the alpine restaurant are at 1,250 m; the pods lie a twenty-minute walk higher, at 1,400 m, reached only on foot.

Montreux is about 45 minutes by car and Geneva Airport about an hour and a half.
