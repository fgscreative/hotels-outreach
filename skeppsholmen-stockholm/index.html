The Place · Skeppsholmen · Stockholm

On the island of Skeppsholmen

Hotel Skeppsholmen occupies two long seventeenth-century buildings on Skeppsholmen, a green island in the middle of Stockholm’s harbour.

Moderna Museet and ArkDes are neighbours; a bridge reaches Blasieholmen and the Nationalmuseum, and the Old Town lies across the water.

Ferries to Djurgården and the archipelago leave from the island’s quays.
