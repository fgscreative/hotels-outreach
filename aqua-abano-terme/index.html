The Place · Abano Terme · Colli Euganei

Spa town of the Euganean Hills

Abano Terme lies at the foot of the Colli Euganei, a ring of extinct volcanoes rising out of the Venetian plain.

Rain falling on the Pre-Alps sinks some 3,000 metres underground and travels for years before surfacing here, hot and rich in salts.

The hotel is a short walk from the pedestrian centre — pool in the morning, espresso on the corso by eleven.
