The Place · Donegal Town · Lough Eske

On Lough Eske

Lough Eske Castle stands on the shore of its lough, a few minutes from Donegal Town, under the Blue Stack Mountains.

It was a seat of the O’Donnell clan; Thomas Brooke rebuilt it in the Victorian-Gothic style in the 1860s, and Oscar Wilde was a guest of the Brookes.

Later ruined by fire, it was restored by Pat Doherty and welcomed its first hotel guests in 2007.
