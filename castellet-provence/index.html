The Place · Le Castellet · Var, Provence

Above Bandol and the bay

The hotel sits at 430 metres in the hills of the Var, between Provence and the Côte d’Azur, beside the Paul Ricard circuit.

Its ochre buildings stand in a twelve-hectare park of umbrella pines, and every room opens onto a private terrace.

Below lie the vineyards of Bandol and the hilltop village of Le Castellet; Marseille is about forty-five minutes away.
