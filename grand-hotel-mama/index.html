The Place · La Maddalena · Sardinia

In the Maddalena archipelago

La Maddalena is the largest of the seven main islands off Sardinia’s north-east coast, a national park of granite and clear water.

The resort sits in 3 ha of maquis at Punta Tegge, 150 m from the sea, facing the island of Santo Stefano.

Ferries cross from Palau every twenty minutes; the port is about 2 km away.
