The Place · Eixample · Barcelona

On the Gran Via

El Palace opened in 1919 as Barcelona’s Ritz, on the Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes in the heart of the Eixample.

Its suites are named for the guests who lived here — Salvador Dalí, Joséphine Baker, César Ritz himself.

Passeig de Gràcia is steps away; Plaça de Catalunya a short walk.
