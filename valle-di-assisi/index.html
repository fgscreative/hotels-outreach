The Place · Assisi · Umbrian valley

Below Assisi

The estate lies on the floor of the Spoleto valley at Santa Maria degli Angeli, below Assisi and the pink stone of Monte Subasio.

Since 2019 the Bianconi family has farmed its 80 hectares organically, pressing its own extra-virgin oil and making its own wine.

Assisi is ten minutes by car; Perugia about twenty.
