The Place · Carnac-Plage · Brittany

Steps from the Grande Plage

Le Celtique stands on the Avenue des Druides in Carnac-Plage, about a hundred metres from the Grande Plage on the Bay of Quiberon.

From the beach the view runs out to the islands of Houat and Belle-Île; inland lie Carnac’s alignments, some 3,000 standing stones.

The hotel has welcomed seaside visitors since the 1920s and reopened in 2021, renovated in the Art Deco spirit of its beginnings.
