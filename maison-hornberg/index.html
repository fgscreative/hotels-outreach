The Place · Saanenmöser · Gstaad

In the Saanenland

Maison Hornberg stands at Saanenmöser, on the pass between Gstaad and Zweisimmen, at 1,270 metres.

The ski slopes and the cross-country trail start beside the hotel; walking and bike paths lead off in every direction.

Gstaad is a few minutes by the GoldenPass line; a golf course is close by.
