The Place · Fowey · Cornwall

Above Fowey harbour

Fowey Hall stands on the hill above the harbour, looking across the estuary to Polruan.

It was built in 1889–99 for Charles Hanson, later Lord Mayor of London. Kenneth Grahame stayed, and the house became Toad Hall; before D-Day it housed the US 29th Division.

The Eden Project and the Lost Gardens of Heligan are close by — this is Daphne du Maurier country.
