The Place · Westport · Clew Bay

On Clew Bay

The Plaza stands on Castlebar Street in the centre of Westport, a planned Georgian town on the Carrowbeg river.

Clew Bay and its islands lie west of the town, with Croagh Patrick rising over the water.

The Great Western Greenway starts here, for walkers and cyclists heading out to Achill.
