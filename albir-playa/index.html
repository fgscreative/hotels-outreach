The Place · El Albir · Costa Blanca

Below the Serra Gelada

El Albir lies between Altea and Benidorm, where the Serra Gelada rises from the sea in a wall of cliffs, now a natural park.

From the end of the beach a path runs about two kilometres along the cliffs to the Albir lighthouse.

Altea is about ten minutes away, Benidorm fifteen and Alicante airport under an hour.
