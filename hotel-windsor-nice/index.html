The Place · Carré d’Or · Nice

In Nice

The WindsoR stands on rue Dalpozzo, a Belle Époque street a few minutes from the Promenade des Anglais and the Baie des Anges.

B. Pachiaudi, an architect of the Eiffel school, built it in 1896. The Redolfi family has kept it since 1942.

The first artist room — room 83, by Joël Ducoroy — opened in 1989.
