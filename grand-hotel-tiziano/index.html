The Place · Lecce · Salento

At Porta Napoli

The Tiziano stands on Viale Porta d’Europa at the entrance to Lecce, beside Belloluogo Park and a short walk from Porta Napoli.

Inside the walls, Lecce’s Baroque churches are carved from the soft, golden local stone.

The Brindisi airport shuttle stops 50 m from the door; both the Adriatic and the Ionian coasts of Salento are within the hour.
