The Place · Amalfi · Costiera Amalfitana

On the Divina Costiera

Hotel Santa Caterina stands on the cliff at the western entrance to Amalfi, on the Amalfitana road, with lemon terraces falling to the sea.

Amalfi’s cathedral square is a short walk away; Ravello, Positano and Sorrento are along the same coast road.

Capri, Pompeii, Herculaneum and Naples are all within a day’s reach by boat or car.
