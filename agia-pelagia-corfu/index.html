The Place · Agia Pelagia · Corfu

On Corfu’s southeast coast

Agia Pelagia lies on the quiet southeast coast of Corfu, between Boukari and Messonghi, below the hill village of Chlomos.

The estate grew around a 19th-century olive press; the first oil was bottled here 180 years ago.

Three motor yachts are available for days along the coast.
