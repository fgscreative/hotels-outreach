The Place · Sierra Norte de Madrid · Spain

In the Sierra Norte

El Bulín’s houses sit in the villages of the Sierra Norte de Madrid — Montejo, Horcajuelo, Prádena, Patones de Arriba — around 1,000 to 1,200 m up.

The Sierra del Rincón is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve; its Hayedo de Montejo is among the southernmost beech forests in Europe and part of a World Heritage site since 2017.

Madrid is about an hour and a quarter by car; more houses wait in Segovia, near walled Pedraza, and in Albacete.
