The Place · Bucharest · Sector 2

Five minutes from the old centre

Hotel Sarroglia stands on Strada Vasile Lascăr, a long street running through the heart of Bucharest’s Sector 2.

Piața Romană and the Grădina Icoanei park are a short walk away; the Romanian Athenaeum and the Old Town are within reach on foot.

Gara de Nord is about three kilometres away, Otopeni airport around half an hour by car.
