The Place · Cap Estate · Saint Lucia

At Cap Estate

Cap Maison sits on the cliffs of Cap Estate at the northern tip of Saint Lucia, above its own sandy beach on Smuggler’s Cove.

From the terraces the view runs north across the Saint Lucia Channel to Martinique.

Rodney Bay is about ten minutes away; George F. L. Charles airport around twenty-five, and Hewanorra about ninety.
