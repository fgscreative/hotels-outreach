The Place · Zola Predosa · Bologna

Under the Bologna hills

Zola Predosa sits where the plain meets the Colli Bolognesi, the hills of Pignoletto wine, beside the Lavino river.

The hotel was once a winery; today its park has three marked trails and a pool facing the hills.

Unipol Arena is minutes away, the Dall’Ara stadium fifteen, BolognaFiere about twenty, and the airport close by.
