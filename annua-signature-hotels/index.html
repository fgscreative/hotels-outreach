The Place · Mallorca · Menorca · Lanzarote

On the Tramuntana coast

Gran Hotel Margalida stands above Banyalbufar, a village on Mallorca’s north-west coast whose dry-stone terraces step down to the sea.

The terraces belong to the Serra de Tramuntana, a cultural landscape listed by UNESCO in 2011 for its farming and old water channels.

From there the collection crosses to Menorca’s olive groves, Lanzarote’s volcanoes and, from 2027, the pine coast of Comporta.
