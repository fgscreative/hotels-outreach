The Place · Elounda · Crete

On the Gulf of Mirabello

Elounda Beach sits on 40 acres of Crete’s north-east coast, its bungalows and villas set right at the waterline of a sheltered bay.

Across the water lies Spinalonga, the Venetian island fortress; behind the hotel, the olive grove climbs towards the hills of Lasithi.

Agios Nikolaos is about nine kilometres away; Heraklion airport roughly an hour by road.
