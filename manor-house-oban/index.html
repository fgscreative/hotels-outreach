The Place · Oban · Argyll

On the south shore of Oban Bay

The Manor House was built in 1780 as the principal residence of the Duke of Argyll’s Oban estate, on the sheltered south shore of the bay.

From the garden the view runs over Oban Bay to Kerrera, Lismore and Mull, with the hills of Morvern beyond.

The town, the ferry, bus and rail terminals are a few minutes’ walk; Oban calls itself the Seafood Capital of Scotland.
