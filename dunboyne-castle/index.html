The Place · Dunboyne · Co. Meath

In County Meath

The Butlers’ castle stood here until the Cromwellian wars. In 1764 the Drogheda architect George Darley finished the house that stands today.

Inside, the Lafranchini brothers’ stucco on the stair ceiling shows War, Music, Education and Love.

Dublin is 18 km away and the airport about twenty; Fairyhouse Racecourse is close by.
