The Place · Cala d'Ambra · San Teodoro

On the sand at Cala d'Ambra

L'Esagono stands directly on Cala d'Ambra, a small bay of pale sand on the north-east coast of Sardinia, 800 metres from San Teodoro.

Around the bungalows, 10,000 m² of Mediterranean park keeps the shade deep and the noise far away.

Offshore rises Tavolara, a limestone island 565 metres high at the heart of a protected marine area.
