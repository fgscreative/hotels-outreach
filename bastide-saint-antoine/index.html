The Place · Grasse · Côte d’Azur

Above Grasse

La Bastide Saint-Antoine stands among olive trees on the hillside of Grasse, the perfume town, with the Bay of Cannes on the horizon.

The 17th-century bastide was a hunting lodge; the Rolling Stones spent a year here in the 1970s.

Odette and Jacques Chibois bought it in 1996. It joined Relais & Châteaux in 2000 and took its fifth star in 2009.
