The Place · Lapa · Lisboa

In Lapa

Palácio Ramalhete stands on Rua das Janelas Verdes in Lapa, one of Lisbon’s oldest residential quarters, beside the Museu Nacional de Arte Antiga.

Its oldest wing dates to the 17th century and came through the great earthquake of 1755 unscathed.

The Tagus waterfront is a short walk downhill; the Baixa and Belém are about ten minutes by tram or taxi.
